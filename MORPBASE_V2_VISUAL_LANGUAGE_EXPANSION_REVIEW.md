# MorpBase V2 Visual Language Expansion Review

## Verdict

- **Pass**

## What Improved

### 1. `Shape The Look` now feels less prototype-thin

The old look field was beginning to feel too narrow beside the stronger subject side.

That is now much weaker.

### 2. The expansion stayed curated

The grouped structure helps a lot.

It feels:

- shaped
- readable
- product-real

and not like:

- a style marketplace
- a giant tag field

### 3. Mood stayed in the right role

Keeping `Mood And Light` broader was the right restraint.

It still helps the preview feel alive, but it does not fight the look field for control.

## Honest Reading

This does not finish the center.

But it restores balance between:

- `Define The Subject`
- and `Shape The Look`

which was the real need.

## Plain Conclusion

The center now feels more even because the look side can carry more range without becoming noisy.
