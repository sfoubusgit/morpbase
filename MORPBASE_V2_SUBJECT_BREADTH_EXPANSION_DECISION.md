# MorpBase V2 Subject-Breadth Expansion Decision

## Chosen Direction

The healthiest first subject-breadth expansion is:

- **a curated 9-option subject field arranged in tonal groups**

## Why This Wins

This is stronger than:

- staying at 3 options
- or jumping to a giant open list

It keeps the center:

- curated
- memorable
- easy to scan

while making it feel:

- clearly broader
- less prototype-thin
- and more believable as the real heart of MorpBase

## The First Expansion Shape

Use three light tonal groups:

1. `Visionary Lines`
2. `Journey Lines`
3. `Force Lines`

With 3 subject options in each group.

That creates:

- 9 total subject options

## Why Grouping Matters

Grouping prevents the subject layer from feeling like:

- one widening chip pile

It helps it feel like:

- a shaped field

without becoming:

- a taxonomy
- a dropdown
- or a subject library

## Honest Conclusion

The first coded subject-breadth slice should broaden `Define The Subject`, but still keep it unmistakably curated.
