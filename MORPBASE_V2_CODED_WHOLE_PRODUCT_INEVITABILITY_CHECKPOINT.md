# MorpBase V2 Coded Whole-Product Inevitability Checkpoint

## 1. What Now Holds

Several important whole-product truths now genuinely hold.

### 1. The center

`Workspace` + `Prompt Preview` + `Keep` now feel like the real heart of MorpBase.

### 2. The engine reading

The product now feels much more like:

- work moving through states of value

and less like:

- feature zones with separate local logic

### 3. The realm family

`Workspace`, `Memory`, `Community`, and `Continuity` now feel like:

- one world
- with different weights and temperaments

### 4. The private-public-return loop

The product now has a believable line of movement:

- make
- keep
- return
- circulate
- recur
- re-enter

That is a real gain.

## 2. What Still Feels Assembled

The biggest remaining whole-product weakness is:

- some secondary support surfaces still carry too much equal weight

Plainly:

- many individual surfaces are now stronger
- but the total product can still feel slightly over-carded
- slightly over-boxed
- and slightly too assembled from many equally “good” support blocks

So the gap is no longer:

- missing meaning

It is:

- **hierarchy inside the total coded experience**

The product now wants:

- clearer distinction between primary and secondary surfaces
- calmer support layers
- and slightly more whole-product breathing room

## 3. What Should Not Happen Next

The wrong next moves would be:

- another narrow signature-surface pass
- new systems
- new public participation features
- heavier motif spread
- or more local enrichments that make the total product denser

That would repeat the old drift pattern in a more polished form.

## 4. Healthiest Tightening Direction

The healthiest next move is:

- **a broader coded hierarchy-and-coherence tightening pass**

The main focus of that pass should be:

### 1. Support-surface hierarchy

Make secondary surfaces feel more clearly secondary.

### 2. Whole-product balance

Reduce the sense that the product is composed of many co-equal boxed layers.

### 3. Realm-family coherence

Make the different realm temperaments continue to feel related without every support surface carrying the same strength.

## 5. Honest Conclusion

The product is now strong enough that the next important move is not more invention.

It is:

- making the current whole feel calmer
- more hierarchically inevitable
- and less assembled from many locally strong pieces
