# MorpBase V2 Trace Family Checkpoint

## What Now Clearly Holds

- V2 now has a real second symbolic family
- `Memory` expresses durable carried value
- `Community` expresses outward travel and readable public consequence
- `Continuity` expresses recurrence through recognizable sameness

The strongest new truth is:

- traces now feel like part of MorpBase itself, not just styling added on top

That matters because the product can now say, more quietly but more clearly:

- something endured
- something travelled
- something stayed recognizable

## What Still Needs Protection

- the trace family must stay quieter than the threshold family
- it must not spread across every panel
- it must not become decorative connector theater
- it must not turn `Community` into social graph visuals
- it must not make `Continuity` feel heavier or more symbolic than the product can support

The biggest protection rule now is:

- traces should still be read mostly on focus and consequence surfaces, not everywhere

## Biggest Remaining Gap

The biggest remaining gap is now:

- **center-side trace calibration**

In plain terms:

- the outer realms now read carried movement better
- but the center could still say a little more clearly what line arrived here and what reading came with it

That does not mean:

- a big new symbolic system in `Workspace`

It means:

- a light calibration around `Prompt Preview`
- arrival reading
- and return-entry cues

## Best Next Move

The healthiest next move is:

- a restrained `Preview / arrival-trace calibration` pass

Why this wins:

- the trace family now already holds across the three support realms
- the next natural bridge is to make the center read incoming carried value a little better
- and that is safer than spreading traces into more surfaces or starting another symbolic family immediately

What should still wait:

- more threshold growth
- trace language on utility surfaces
- broader symbolic ornament

## Honest Conclusion

`After the three trace passes, MorpBase V2 now has a believable trace family that supports endurance, travel, and recurrence. The healthiest next move is not to spread it further, but to lightly calibrate the center so carried value is read more clearly when work returns into live creation.`
