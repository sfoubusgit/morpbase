# MorpBase V2 Support-Surface Calmness Tightening Brief

## Intent

This pass should make the live product feel less:

- stacked
- boxed
- and support-heavy

without changing product structure.

## Scope

Touch only:

- support bands
- support notes
- support separators
- local spacing and surface weight where stacked support panels feel too explicit

Do not touch:

- product structure
- major realm layout
- object model
- feature scope

## Main Goal

Keep the strongest readings,

but reduce the feeling that the product is:

- several good explanatory modules placed beside each other

The product should feel:

- calmer
- more breathable
- more inevitable

## Honest Standard

If this works, the live product should feel more like one held product-world and less like a very strong prototype with many helpful bands.
