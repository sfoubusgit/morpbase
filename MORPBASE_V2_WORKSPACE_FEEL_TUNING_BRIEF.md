# MorpBase V2 Workspace Feel-Tuning Brief

## Goal

Make the `Workspace` feel more like guided movement and less like all phases are equally open at the same time.

## Chosen Direction

The strongest restrained feel-tuning move is:

- one clearer active phase
- calmer collapsed non-active phases
- explicit but light re-entry
- clearer current-focus guidance near the center and Preview

## What This Means In Practice

- only one phase should read as fully open at a time
- completed and upcoming phases should compress into calm summaries
- users should still be able to revisit phases intentionally
- Preview should reflect the current creative focus more clearly
- the keep threshold should stay visible without feeling administrative

## What Must Stay True

- the center stays the center
- the flow stays non-wizard
- the Workspace does not become denser
- the product still feels calm and product-real
