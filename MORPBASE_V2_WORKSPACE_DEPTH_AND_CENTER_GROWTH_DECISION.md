# MorpBase V2 Workspace Depth And Center-Growth Decision

## 1. The Real Center Problem

The problem is not that `Workspace` is bad now.

The problem is that it is still **too thin for the role it now has to carry**.

Plainly:

- the product shell is stronger
- `Memory`, `Community`, and `Continuity` now feel more believable
- but the center still reads a little too much like a clean first slice

So the gap is:

- `the coded Workspace still feels more like a good starting structure than the true heart of MorpBase`

That means the next growth step should not be:

- more edge systems
- more public complexity
- more decorative design

It should be:

- a more believable creative rhythm at the center

## 2. Real-Life Usage Drafts

### Draft 1. First-time user shaping a portrait

What they need:

- a clear sense of movement
- a feeling that each step is a meaningful creative decision
- early trust in the Preview

What is weak in the current Workspace:

- it works
- but the middle still feels slightly too flat
- there is not yet enough sense of distinct creative moves

What would help most:

- one stronger subject-defining move beyond simple subject choice
- one stronger staging/presentation move beyond look and mood alone
- a clearer sense of where they are in the overall flow

### Draft 2. Returning user continuing something from Memory

What they need:

- faster re-entry
- clearer local moves
- a sense that they are reopening a real workflow, not just a saved prompt

What is weak in the current Workspace:

- the return loop is good
- but the center still does not offer enough distinct reshaping points

What would help most:

- blocks that let the user change the subject's felt presence
- blocks that let the user reshape how the image is framed or emphasized

### Draft 3. User comparing MorpBase to simpler prompt tools

What they need to feel:

- MorpBase is more helpful, not just more structured

What is risky in the current Workspace:

- if the center stays too lean for too long, the rest of the product can start feeling more advanced than the creation heart itself

What would help most:

- a stronger guided workflow reading
- without turning the center into a bigger form

## 3. Candidate Comparison

### Candidate A. Add more shaping blocks without changing the flow

Good:

- easy to implement
- makes the center richer quickly

Bad:

- risks feeling like more cards in the same lane
- does not solve the deeper problem of workflow rhythm

Judgment:

- useful, but not enough by itself

### Candidate B. Add a light phase rhythm plus the highest-value missing blocks

Good:

- deepens the center without going full heavy
- makes the Workspace feel more like a real workflow
- supports both first-time entry and return use
- stays close to the long-term V2 center model without jumping there too fast

Bad:

- requires discipline so phase language stays light

Judgment:

- strongest option

### Candidate C. Make Preview deeper instead of the left side

Good:

- preserves the trust surface
- keeps the center visually lighter

Bad:

- the real gap is not only on the right side
- Preview is already doing its job much better now
- this would postpone the actual Workspace problem

Judgment:

- not the best next move

### Candidate D. Make Memory-to-Workspace continuation deeper instead

Good:

- continuation is very important

Bad:

- the center itself would still remain too thin
- this helps return use more than the heart of the product overall

Judgment:

- worth revisiting later, but not the best current move

### Candidate E. Go straight to the full long-term Workspace model

Good:

- ambitious
- could close the center gap quickly

Bad:

- too much at once
- higher drift risk
- more likely to turn into a disguised big-form expansion

Judgment:

- too early

## 4. Chosen Direction

The strongest next move is:

- **a light phase-guided Workspace foundation plus two high-value missing shaping blocks**

This means:

- introduce a visible but calm 4-phase reading
- deepen the current Workspace with the most important missing moves
- do not add the full final block system yet

The two best missing blocks to add now are:

1. `Presence And Signature`
2. `Framing And Focus`

Why these two:

- `Presence And Signature` makes the subject feel more particular, not just selected
- `Framing And Focus` makes the image feel more authored, not just styled

Those two additions deepen the center in the most meaningful way without making it heavy.

## 5. What The Next Coded Slice Should Be

The next coded slice should:

- keep `Prompt Preview` as the trust surface
- keep `Keep` on the Preview side
- introduce a light visible 4-phase rhythm:
  1. `Define The Subject`
  2. `Shape The Look`
  3. `Stage The Image`
  4. `Refine And Keep`
- add:
  - `Presence And Signature`
  - `Framing And Focus`
- keep:
  - `Subject Core`
  - `Visual Direction`
  - `Mood And Light`
  - `Tighten The Result`

So the next Workspace becomes a **6-block center slice**:

1. Subject Core
2. Presence And Signature
3. Visual Direction
4. Mood And Light
5. Framing And Focus
6. Tighten The Result

with:

- phase rhythm above them
- Preview-side `Keep`

## 6. What Should Still Wait

These should still wait:

- `Scene Pressure`
- deeper world/staging logic
- challenge/storyline behavior
- rewards
- bigger collaboration systems
- heavier publishing logic

Why:

- the center still needs cleaner depth first
- not wider ambition

## 7. Honest Conclusion

The next controlled center-growth slice should not be “more Workspace features.” It should be a **more believable workflow center**. The strongest way to do that now is to add a light phase rhythm and the two highest-value missing shaping moves, `Presence And Signature` and `Framing And Focus`, while keeping `Prompt Preview` and `Keep` exactly where their trust role already works.
