# MorpBase V2 Design-Character Tightening Review

## Verdict

`Pass`

## What Improved

- the shell now feels more like a real authored product frame and less like a clean prototype wrapper
- realm atmosphere is stronger, so `Workspace`, `Memory`, `Community`, and `Continuity` feel more like one world with different weights
- the engine strip now reads more clearly as part of the product identity, not just a helpful status row
- major focus surfaces feel more like members of one family
- the product now carries more of a `crafted instrument` feeling and less of a `correct app` feeling

The strongest success is:

- the product now feels more unmistakably like MorpBase without pretending it has already reached the final design universe

## What Still Feels Early

- the full design universe is still not here yet
- the current typography/iconography layer is still only a coded base, not the final signature language
- `Workspace` still remains deliberately narrow in actual creation depth
- the public side is now better held by the shell, but it is still rich enough that future growth needs stronger discipline than ever

## Best Next Move

The healthiest next move is:

- **return to the center**

Meaning:

- do not add another public/community growth feature next
- do not start challenges, rewards, or storylines
- instead, decide the next controlled `Workspace` growth slice from the stronger base we now have

That is healthier because:

- the product edges have grown meaningfully
- the shell now holds them better
- and the next real strength gain should come from deepening the center rather than broadening the orbit again
