# MorpBase V2 Workspace Center-Growth Review

## Result

`Pass`

## What Improved

- the center now reads more clearly as a workflow
- the new phase rhythm gives the Workspace a better sense of movement
- `Presence And Signature` makes the subject feel more particular
- `Framing And Focus` makes the line feel more authored before `Keep`
- `Prompt Preview` still keeps the trust role because `Keep` stayed on the right side

The strongest improvement is:

- the Workspace now feels less like a clean first slice and more like the beginning of the real MorpBase heart

## What Stayed Healthy

- the center did not become too dense
- the new depth still feels controlled
- the slice stayed smaller than the full long-term Workspace model
- `Scene Pressure` and heavier staging still stayed out

## What Still Needs Watching

- the phase rhythm must stay helpful, not instructional
- the center should not keep growing by just adding more blocks
- the next Workspace gain should come from quality of movement, not raw interface weight

## Honest Conclusion

This slice improved the center in the right way. It gave the Workspace more believable creative rhythm and two stronger shaping moves without weakening Preview or making the product feel heavier too fast.
