# MorpBase V2 Phase D Gate 4 Review Prompt

## Purpose

Review the `Phase D` Continuity prototype against its actual job.

Do not judge it by:

- polish
- completeness
- lore potential

Judge it by one question:

- does `Continuity` feel like a real MorpBase realm of recurring sameness with a meaningful return into `Workspace`?

## Required Inputs

Use:

- `MORPBASE_V2_PHASE_D_CONTINUITY_PROTOTYPE_BRIEF.md`
- `MORPBASE_V2_PHASE_D_CONTINUITY_FLOW_MAP.md`
- `MORPBASE_V2_CONTINUITY_REALM_MODEL_ANALYSIS.md`
- `MORPBASE_V2_IMAGE_ROLE_ANALYSIS.md`
- `v2-prototypes/phase-d-continuity/index.html`
- `v2-prototypes/phase-d-continuity/styles.css`
- `v2-prototypes/phase-d-continuity/script.js`

## Review Criteria

Judge the slice on:

1. realm legitimacy
2. entity distinctness
3. appearance/lineage clarity
4. image role discipline
5. activation payoff
6. correct product weighting
7. whether Continuity still feels too much like identity storage

## Output

Write:

- `MORPBASE_V2_PHASE_D_GATE_4_REVIEW.md`

The review must include:

1. verdict
2. what clearly works
3. what still feels wrong or weak
4. the biggest risk if we move on too early
5. whether the slice should pass, conditionally pass, or iterate
6. the exact next move
