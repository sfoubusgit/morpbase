# MorpBase V2 After Slice 4 Stage Checkpoint Prompt

## Goal

Pause after the Slice 4 coherence tightening pass and reassess the coded V2 from a broader scope.

This checkpoint must answer:

- what now clearly holds at the whole-product level?
- what still needs protection?
- what is the healthiest next move after the coherence pass?

## Scope

Ground the checkpoint in:

- the frozen V2 foundation
- the post-prototype reconsolidation reading
- the whole-product coded synthesis review
- the current coded V2 app
- the Slice 4 coherence tightening brief

Also keep these future pressures in mind:

- sharability
- collaboration
- playfulness
- challenges
- storylines
- meaningful reward

## Questions

### 1. Product Truth

- Does the coded V2 now feel more like one integrated MorpBase engine?
- Does the product hierarchy still hold?
- Is the return-loop law still strong?

### 2. Current Risk

- What is now the biggest remaining weakness?
- Is it structure, interaction, thinness, visual character, or something else?

### 3. Expansion Discipline

- Should we add another system next?
- Or is there a more important strengthening phase first?

### 4. Best Next Move

- What is the single healthiest next move now?
- Why is that the right next layer?

## Output Format

Write the result as:

1. `What Now Clearly Holds`
2. `What Still Needs Protection`
3. `Biggest Remaining Gap`
4. `Best Next Move`

## Guardrails

- do not recommend a new system just because more is possible
- prefer coherence over momentum
- keep the heart of MorpBase visible
- treat future pressures as preserved horizons, not as automatic next features
