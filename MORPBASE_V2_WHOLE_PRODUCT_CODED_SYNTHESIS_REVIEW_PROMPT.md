# MorpBase V2 Whole-Product Coded Synthesis Review Prompt

## Goal

Reassess the **current coded V2 product as a whole**, not just the planning stack.

This review must answer:

- does the actual coded V2 now feel like the right MorpBase product?
- what clearly works at the whole-product level?
- what still feels weak, thin, generic, or too prototype-like?
- what should be corrected before any new expansion happens?

## Scope

Ground the review in:

- the frozen V2 foundation
- the post-prototype reconsolidation reading
- the after-slice-3 checkpoint
- the current coded app in `morpbase-v2/src/App.tsx`
- the current coded visual layer in `morpbase-v2/src/styles.css`

Also verify whether the current V2 app still passes:

- TypeScript type check
- production build

## Review Frame

Judge the coded V2 as:

- one workflow-centered creative engine
- with `Memory`, `Community`, and `Continuity` as return systems around the same center

Do **not** judge it as:

- four equal realms
- a feature checklist
- a design-final product

## Questions To Answer

### 1. Whole-Product Truth

- Does the coded V2 preserve the heart of MorpBase?
- Does `Workspace` still feel like the unquestioned center?
- Does the total product read as one integrated engine?

### 2. Realm Strength

- Is `Memory` clearly the strongest second home?
- Does `Community` feel like public life growing out of real work?
- Does `Continuity` feel real without becoming heavy?

### 3. Integration

- Do the loops between realms feel meaningful?
- Does the product feel like connected value-return paths rather than separate screens?
- Is `Memory` behaving like the real bridge layer?

### 4. Product Feel

- What feels strong and product-real already?
- What still feels too thin, too explained, too generic, or too prototype-like?
- Where is the coded V2 most at risk of drifting the wrong way if we expand too soon?

### 5. Next Move Discipline

- Should we expand into another system now?
- Or should we do a synthesis tightening pass first?
- What exactly is the healthiest next move?

## Output Format

Write the result as a short governing review with:

1. `Verdict`
2. `What Clearly Works`
3. `What Still Feels Weak`
4. `Corrections To Lock Now`
5. `What Not To Do Next`
6. `Best Next Move`

## Important Guardrails

- Be honest, not flattering
- prefer correction over momentum
- do not reopen the frozen V2 foundation unless there is a serious contradiction
- do not recommend new feature growth just because it is possible
- keep the heart of MorpBase in view at all times
