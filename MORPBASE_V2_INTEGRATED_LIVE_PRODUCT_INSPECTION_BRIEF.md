# MorpBase V2 Integrated Live Product Inspection Brief

## Purpose

Inspect the current V2 as the active integrated baseline.

The goal is not to prove it is finished.

The goal is to judge:

- what now truly feels like MorpBase
- what still feels slightly too prototype-like
- and whether any selective tightening is actually needed

## Main Inspection Questions

### 1. Does the whole product now feel held?

Not just:

- correct

But:

- genuinely like one product

### 2. Does the center still clearly win?

After all the integration work:

- does `Workspace` still feel unquestionably central?

### 3. Do the support realms now belong without competing?

Judge:

- `Memory`
- `Community`
- `Continuity`

### 4. Does the product breathe enough?

Look for:

- too boxed
- too panelized
- too many support bands
- or too much “explaining” still visible

### 5. What is the most surgical next tightening target?

Not:

- broad redesign
- new features
- more symbolic spread

But:

- one real targeted improvement

## Honest Goal

Leave this step with:

- a protected reading of what already works
- and one precise tightening decision only if it is truly justified
