# MorpBase V2 Current-State Freeze And Inspection Brief

## Goal

Pause growth briefly and judge the coded V2 as one whole product.

## Why This Is The Right Move

The product now has enough real working logic that another immediate slice would be riskier than useful.

The right question now is:

- what actually feels strong
- what still feels too prototype-like
- what feels generic
- what feels most like MorpBase

## What This Phase Should Do

### 1. Freeze new growth briefly

No new feature slices during this inspection pass.

### 2. Inspect the real product loop

Judge:

- `Workspace`
- `Memory`
- `Community`
- `Continuity`
- shell balance

### 3. Capture findings in plain product language

Examples:

- too app-like
- too static
- still too prototype-like
- strong and worth preserving
- feels most like MorpBase

### 4. Decide the next true frontier only after inspection

Possible outcomes later:

- another design-character phase
- a new controlled growth slice
- a freeze/packaging step
- implementation hardening

## Success Condition

This phase succeeds if the next move comes from whole-product truth instead of habit or momentum.
