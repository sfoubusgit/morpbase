# MorpBase V2 Broader Center Inspection Prompt

Inspect the `Workspace` again after the first subject-breadth expansion.

Do not ask whether the product works overall.

Ask:

- what the broader subject field actually changed at the center
- what it solved
- and what the next healthy center move is now

## What To Decide

1. Did the broader subject field solve the earlier thinness?
2. What now feels stronger in `Define The Subject`?
3. What still feels too archetype-led or prototype-like at the center?
4. What is the single healthiest next center move?
5. What should still wait?

## Main Protection

Do not let “broader subject field worked” become a reason to keep widening options blindly.

The inspection should be allowed to conclude:

- broader is enough for now
- or one very specific next center move is justified

## Honest Standard

If this works, MorpBase should leave this step with:

- a cleaner reading of what the broader center solved
- and one precise next center move, not vague “more depth”
