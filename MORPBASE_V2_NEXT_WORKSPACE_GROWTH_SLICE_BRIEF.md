# MorpBase V2 Next Workspace Growth Slice Brief

## 1. Purpose

Deepen the coded `Workspace` so it feels more like the real heart of MorpBase and less like a clean early slice.

## 2. Slice Goal

Add the smallest amount of center depth that creates a stronger creative rhythm.

This slice should prove that:

- `Workspace` can feel more like guided workflow authoring
- without becoming a dense form
- and without weakening `Prompt Preview` as the trust surface

## 3. What This Slice Must Add

### A. Light phase rhythm

Show the coded Workspace as four creative movements:

1. `Define The Subject`
2. `Shape The Look`
3. `Stage The Image`
4. `Refine And Keep`

This should feel:

- visible
- helpful
- and calm

Not:

- wizard-like
- heavy
- or over-instructional

### B. Two high-value shaping blocks

Add:

1. `Presence And Signature`
2. `Framing And Focus`

These should feel like meaningful creative moves, not just more settings.

## 4. Workspace Block Shape After This Slice

The Workspace should then read as:

### Phase 1. Define The Subject

- Subject Core
- Presence And Signature

### Phase 2. Shape The Look

- Visual Direction
- Mood And Light

### Phase 3. Stage The Image

- Framing And Focus

### Phase 4. Refine And Keep

- Tighten The Result
- Preview-side Keep handoff

## 5. Interaction Rules

- required blocks should still feel light
- `Subject Core`, `Visual Direction`, and `Framing And Focus` should carry strong structural value
- `Mood And Light` and `Presence And Signature` should make the result feel more particular
- `Tighten The Result` should remain the light refinement move
- `Keep` stays beside Preview, not in the left column

## 6. Product Feel Targets

After this slice, the center should feel:

- more guided
- more authored
- more distinctly MorpBase
- less like a cleaned-up form

## 7. What Must Still Stay Out

- Scene Pressure
- broader setting/world blocks
- deeper collaboration behavior
- storyline logic
- challenge logic
- reward logic

## 8. Success Test

This slice succeeds if the plain product reading becomes:

- `I can feel the workflow better now`

and not:

- `there are more controls now`
