# MorpBase V2 Post-Validation Live Inspection Pack Brief

Goal:
- define the next live whole-product inspection after the first validation round

What this inspection should focus on:
- does the whole product now feel more inevitable
- does the center still clearly dominate without flattening the support realms
- do the three return systems still feel distinct but integrated
- does anything still feel too app-like, too neat, or too theoretical

Best inspection lenses:
- center strength
- support-realm balance
- public inward meaning
- continuity payoff
- overall authored product feel

Guardrails:
- keep it whole-product
- use plain product questions
- only open a tuning pass if something feels directly wrong
