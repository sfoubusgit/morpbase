# MorpBase V2 Continuity Recurrence-Trace Pass Review

## Verdict

- **Pass**

## What Improved

`Continuity` now feels more like:

- a readable recurring line

and less like:

- a well-explained identity grouping that still needs extra interpretation

### 1. The continuity focus now says more clearly that recurrence leaves a trace

The new recurrence-trace band helps the surface say:

- this line now leaves readable sameness behind

That is more native to MorpBase than only showing:

- appearance count
- carried signals
- and explanation text

### 2. Recognizability now reads more clearly than repetition

Changing the emphasis from:

- `What Repeats`

to:

- `What Stays Recognizable`

helps the area feel more like:

- lived sameness

and less like:

- abstract recurrence accounting

### 3. Continuity stayed light

This mattered.

The pass did not make `Continuity` feel:

- heavier
- more mystical
- or more management-like

It still feels:

- fine-lined
- quieter
- and recurrence-led

## What Still Is Not Final

This is still the coded bridge, not the final custom trace family.

The later Illustrator-backed layer will still want:

- finer trace fragments
- quieter recurrence accents
- and better symbolic line quality

## What This Pass Did Not Break

Important:

- `Continuity` did not become louder
- it did not drift toward story-system theater
- the trace family still stays quieter than the threshold family

## Recommended Next Move

- **Trace-family checkpoint and consolidation**

Why:

- `Memory` now expresses endurance
- `Community` now expresses outward travel and consequence
- `Continuity` now expresses readable recurrence

So the healthiest next step is to judge whether the whole trace family now truly holds across the product before spreading it anywhere else.

## Honest Conclusion

This was the right third trace-family bridge because it made recurrence feel more native to MorpBase without making `Continuity` feel heavier than it should.
