# MorpBase V2 Memory-To-Workspace Re-Entry Review

## Result

`Pass`

- return into `Workspace` now feels more intentional
- the product now explains why you arrived, not just where you are
- the best next move is clearer without becoming rigid
- the core loop now feels more like continuation and less like a reload

The most important gain is:

- the engine understands the reason for return, not just the fact of return

- the center stayed calm
- no new system was added
- the flow did not become wizard-like
- users can still deliberately revisit any phase

## What Still Needs Watching

- the product should not start narrating too much
- future return-loop improvements should still be about feel, not interface weight

## Honest Conclusion
