# MorpBase V2 Post-Inevitability Freeze And Inspection Prompt

You are pausing MorpBase V2 after a substantial coded inevitability phase.

This is a real stage boundary.

The product now has:

- a real coded center
- `Workspace`, `Memory`, `Community`, and `Continuity`
- a completed signature-surface chain
- and a broader whole-product hierarchy/coherence tightening pass

Your job is to stop, reassess, and decide what should now be frozen, protected, and inspected before any more growth or even more tightening happens.

This is not a "what new thing can we build next?" prompt.
It is a product-judgment prompt.

## What You Must Decide

### 1. What now feels genuinely real

Do not describe what is merely present.
Describe what now feels like a believable part of the real MorpBase V2 product.

### 2. What still does not feel final

Be honest about what still feels:

- prototype-like
- not fully inevitable
- or still waiting for a later stronger design-universe layer

### 3. What should now be frozen and protected

Identify the truths that should not be casually changed in the next phase.

### 4. What should definitely not happen next

Protect against:

- more local overgrowth
- more public feature stacking
- new systems
- or endless coded micro-polish

### 5. What the healthiest next phase is

Choose whether the next move should be:

- live inspection
- design-universe execution preparation
- temporary freeze
- implementation hardening
- or something else

## Required Output Shape

Write a concrete stage judgment with sections like:

1. `What Now Feels Real`
2. `What Still Feels Provisional`
3. `What Should Be Frozen`
4. `What Should Not Happen Next`
5. `Healthiest Next Phase`
6. `Honest Conclusion`
