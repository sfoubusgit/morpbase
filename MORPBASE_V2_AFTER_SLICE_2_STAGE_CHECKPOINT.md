# MorpBase V2 After Slice 2 Stage Checkpoint

## What Now Clearly Holds

- V2 now feels much more like one integrated MorpBase engine.
- the private loop works:
  - `Workspace -> Keep -> Memory -> return`
- the first public loop works:
  - `Memory -> Community -> Memory`
- `Memory` is now clearly the bridge between private creation and public circulation
- `Community` no longer feels like a detached idea

## What Still Needs Protection

- the product center must stay protected from public-layer expansion
- `Community` must continue to feel object-led, not feed-led
- `Memory` must remain a real second home, not just a launchpad
- future reward and collaboration ideas must still grow out of real object use, not generic engagement

## Best Next Move

`Move to Continuity as the next implementation slice`

## Why Now

This is now the right moment for `Continuity` because:

- the private core is strong enough
- sharability is already proven enough for the moment
- the next major missing value-return system is recurring sameness across work
- `Continuity` can now attach to a stronger engine instead of competing with a weak center

It is also the right timing because it prepares the ground for several future pressures without building them yet:

- deeper identity value
- more meaningful collaboration later
- storyline and playfulness later
- more product-native forms of reward later
