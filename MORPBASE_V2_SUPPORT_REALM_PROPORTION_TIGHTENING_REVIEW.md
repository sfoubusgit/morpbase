# MorpBase V2 Support Realm Proportion Tightening Review

## Verdict

`Pass`

## Plain Reading

This pass makes `Memory`, `Community`, and `Continuity` feel calmer and more proportionate without making them feel weak.

## What Improved

- support-realm shells now pull a little less attention
- support focus surfaces feel quieter and less equally forceful beside `Workspace`
- banners, prompt blocks, and supporting panels feel less boxed and less declarative
- `Community` and `Continuity` still read clearly, but they do not compete as hard with the center

## What Stayed Protected

- `Memory` still feels like a real second home
- `Community` still feels like the public life of real work
- `Continuity` still feels like recurrence, not storage
- the integrated shell / threshold / trace baseline remains intact

## Overall Judgment

This is the right kind of tightening pass:

- proportional
- design-led
- non-destructive

The product should now read more clearly as:

- `Workspace` center first
- support realms second
