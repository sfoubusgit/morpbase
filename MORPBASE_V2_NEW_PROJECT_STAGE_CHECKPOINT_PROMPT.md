# MorpBase V2 New Project Stage Checkpoint Prompt

Reassess the whole situation after the creation of the fresh `morpbase-v2` project folder and the first tightened Slice 1 implementation.

Do not just check whether the code compiles. Re-check the broader product truth.

Answer:

`What have we actually proven, what still feels weak, and what is the healthiest next move from here?`

## Review from a broader scope

1. Does the new V2 project already preserve the heart of MorpBase?
2. Does Slice 1 truly feel like the beginning of one workflow-centered engine?
3. Are there any signs of generic scaffolding, wrong hierarchy, or early drift?
4. Would jumping to Slice 2 now be disciplined, or premature?
5. What parts should be recombined, tightened, or protected before moving forward?
6. What is the best next move:
   - harden Slice 1
   - start Slice 2
   - pause for another design/product correction

## Output

Write:

`MORPBASE_V2_NEW_PROJECT_STAGE_CHECKPOINT.md`

Include:

- What now clearly holds
- What still needs protection or correction
- Best next move
- Why that move is the right timing
