# MorpBase V2 New Project Stage Checkpoint

## What Now Clearly Holds

- The V2 rebuild is no longer theoretical.
  - there is now a real fresh codebase in its own folder
- The heart of MorpBase survived the restart.
  - `Workspace` is the center
  - `Prompt Preview` is part of the live loop
  - `Keep` matters
  - `Memory` already feels like a second home more than storage
- Slice 1 already reads as the beginning of one workflow-centered engine.
  - not as detached screens
  - not as a V1 repaint

## What Still Needs Protection Or Correction

- Slice 1 is promising, but still young.
  - it proves the loop
  - it does not yet prove depth, durability, or continuation quality
- The shell hierarchy is much better now, but it still needs to be protected from future flattening.
- It would be easy to jump too quickly toward `Community` or `Continuity` just because those realms already exist in the blueprint.
  - that would risk repeating the V1 pattern of broadening before the center becomes strong enough

## Best Next Move

`Harden Slice 1 before starting Slice 2`

## Why That Timing Is Right

This is the moment where discipline matters most.

We have already proven that the fresh V2 start works. What we have not yet proven is whether the first slice can carry a stronger, more believable continuation loop:

- better kept-work depth
- stronger reopen / branch behavior
- stronger session-to-memory continuity
- stronger feeling that good work can actually stay alive here

If that hardening succeeds, then later slices will connect to a stronger center. If it is skipped, later realms could become impressive but less grounded.

So the right move is not to pause indefinitely and not to jump ahead.

The right move is:

`one disciplined Slice 1 hardening phase`
