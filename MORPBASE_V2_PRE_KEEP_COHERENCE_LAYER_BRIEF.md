# MorpBase V2 Pre Keep Coherence Layer Brief

## Intent

This step should make `Refine And Keep` feel less compressed.

## Problem

The center is now stronger in:

- subject
- visual language
- staging

So the next narrowness is easier to feel in `Phase 4`, which currently relies too heavily on:

- one finishing note
- and the separate keep surface

## Scope

Design only:

- the first light `pre-keep coherence` layer inside `Phase 4`

Do not design:

- a scoring system
- threshold bureaucracy
- new workflow phases
- more subject, look, or staging growth
- edge-system growth

## Main Goal

Keep `Refine And Keep`:

- calm
- compact
- reflective

But make it:

- more meaningful
- more believable
- and a stronger bridge into `Memory`

## Honest Standard

If this works, the next coded center slice should make `Refine And Keep` feel:

- like the final gathering point before crossing

instead of:

- one small note beside a stronger keep panel
