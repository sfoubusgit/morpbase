# MorpBase V2 Subject-Breadth Expansion Brief

## Intent

This step should broaden the center in the most controlled way possible.

The problem is not:

- that the current subject options are wrong

The problem is:

- they are still too narrow for the role the center now has to carry

## Scope

Design only:

- the next subject-option expansion slice for `Define The Subject`

Do not design:

- giant subject taxonomies
- world-building systems
- new public object types
- continuity growth

## Main Goal

Keep the subject layer:

- curated
- memorable
- and easy to enter

But make it:

- clearly broader than the current three-option starter set

## Honest Standard

If this works, the next coded center slice should feel:

- more alive
- more believable
- and less visibly prototype-thin
