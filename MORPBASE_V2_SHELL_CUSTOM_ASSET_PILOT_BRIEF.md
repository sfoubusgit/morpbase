# MorpBase V2 Shell Custom Asset Pilot Brief

## Intent

This pilot should prove that MorpBase can begin to wear a more custom shell language without losing:

- clarity
- hierarchy
- restraint

## Scope

Touch only:

- top-frame joins
- one brand-anchor support fragment
- one engine-strip join family
- one subtle primary-nav structural cue

Do not turn this into:

- a general shell redesign
- a new symbolic family
- realm-specific art
- or decorative premium polish

## Desired Feeling

The result should feel:

- more authored
- more singular
- more product-real

but not:

- louder
- heavier
- or more ornate

## Honest Standard

If this pilot works, the top of MorpBase should feel more like:

- one crafted product frame

and less like:

- a strong coded shell still waiting for its first real custom layer
