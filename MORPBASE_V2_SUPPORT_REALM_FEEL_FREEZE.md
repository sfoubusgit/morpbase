# MorpBase V2 Support-Realm Feel Freeze

Result:
- `Frozen`

What is now frozen:
- `Memory` should feel like a second home, not storage
- `Community` should feel object-led, not feed-led
- `Continuity` should feel light but real, not abstract and not managerial
- all three should clearly support the stronger `Workspace` center

What this freeze protects:
- support-surface calmness
- support-realm integration into one engine
- asymmetry between the center and the support realms

What selective tuning still allows:
- calmness tightening if a support surface grows too loud
- hierarchy tightening if a support realm pulls too evenly against `Workspace`
- wording cleanup if a realm starts sounding generic or mechanical

What this freeze blocks:
- new support-side features by momentum
- broader public-system growth by reflex
- heavier continuity behavior without a new checkpoint

Conclusion:
- the support realms are now stable enough to protect
- future changes should justify themselves against this frozen reading
