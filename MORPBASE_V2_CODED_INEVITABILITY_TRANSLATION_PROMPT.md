# MorpBase V2 Coded Inevitability Translation Prompt

You are translating the current `whole-product inevitability` stack into the **next coded design pass** for MorpBase V2.

This is not a feature-growth task.
It is a product-character translation task.

Your job is to determine:

- what the first coded inevitability pass should actually change
- which parts of the live V2 matter most in this pass
- what should become more singular now
- and what should still wait

---

## Product Truth To Protect

MorpBase V2 is:

- one workflow-centered creative engine
- with `Workspace` as the center
- `Memory` as the second home
- `Community` as public circulation
- `Continuity` as a lighter recurrence-aware realm

The product is already structurally strong enough.

The current gap is not:

- lack of systems
- lack of flows
- or lack of product logic

The current gap is:

- inevitability
- shell/world feeling
- signature-surface presence
- and deeper authored product character

---

- the shell should become a stronger product-world frame
- the realms should behave like one material family with four temperaments
- only a smaller signature set should carry the strongest MorpBase identity
- the symbolic layer should stay restrained and product-native
- the right symbolic foundation is:
  - traces

  - carried marks

1. `Workspace`
2. `Prompt Preview`

- symbolism becoming theater
