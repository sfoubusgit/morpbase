# MorpBase V2 Creator Grounding Slice Brief

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this slice is:

- to make `Community` feel like the public life of real creators and real practice

In plain terms:

- public objects should no longer feel anonymous
- they should feel attached to a recognizable creative source

This is important because:

- it makes sharing more meaningful
- it makes public work more trustworthy
- it gives later collaboration a human anchor
- and it helps Community feel more alive without becoming noisy

## 2. What Creator Grounding Should Mean

Creator grounding in MorpBase should mean:

- a real creator identity
- a short practice reading
- public work understood through a creator

It should not mean:

- generic social profiles
- personality-first pages
- feed behavior

The right feeling is:

- this work comes from a real creative practice

not:

- this is content posted by a user account

## 3. Where It Should Show First

The first surfaces should be:

### 1. Public Object Focus Surfaces

Because this is where the user most naturally asks:

- who made this?
- is there more from them?

### 2. A First Creator Focus Surface Inside Community

Not a full complex creator system.

Just enough to prove:

- creator identity
- creator practice
- creator objects

## 4. What The First Slice Should Add

The first coded slice should add:

- a calm creator identity strip on public object focus surfaces
- a first creator focus area in Community
- a simple creator practice reading
- object-grounded creator navigation

That means:

- open a public result
- see who made it
- move to a creator view
- understand their public practice through their objects

## 5. What It Must Not Become

This slice must not become:

- a full profile platform
- a social feed
- follower mechanics everywhere
- biography-heavy pages
- generic creator stats theater

If the creator becomes more important than the objects, the slice has drifted.

## 6. Success Criteria

This slice succeeds if:

- Community feels more human
- public objects feel less anonymous
- creator identity adds meaning without noise
- public practice becomes more visible

This slice fails if:

- Community starts feeling profile-first
- the product becomes more social than useful
- or the object engine gets weaker

## Honest Recommendation

This should be the next controlled V2 growth slice.

It is stronger than:

- jumping to collaboration
- jumping to playfulness/challenges
- or inflating publishing mechanics too early
