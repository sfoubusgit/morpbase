# MorpBase V2 Signature Surface Chain Checkpoint Prompt

You are at a real stage boundary in MorpBase V2.

The major signature-surface chain has now been completed:

- `Workspace`
- `Prompt Preview`
- `Keep`
- kept-work focus in `Memory`
- public result focus in `Community`
- continuity focus in `Continuity`

Your job is to step back and reassess the whole coded product from a broader scope.

This is not a "what feature next?" prompt.
It is a coherence checkpoint.

## What You Must Decide

### 1. Has the signature-surface chain actually strengthened MorpBase as a whole?

Do not assume yes.
Check whether the product now feels:

- more singular
- more inevitable
- more like one engine

or whether some local gains may have made the total experience heavier.

### 2. What now feels strongest?

Identify what parts of coded V2 now truly feel right.

### 3. What still feels weakest?

Identify the most important remaining whole-product gap.

### 4. What should happen next?

Decide whether the right next move is:

- another local surface pass
- a broader coherence pass
- a shell/family tightening pass
- a design-universe translation pass
- or a controlled pause/freeze

### 5. Protect against drift

Specifically check whether continuing to stack local improvements now would risk:

- overgrowth
- fragmentation
- over-designed surfaces
- or repeating V1-style drift

## Required Output Shape

Write a concrete checkpoint with sections like:

1. `What The Signature-Surface Chain Achieved`
2. `What Now Feels Strongest`
3. `What Still Feels Weakest`
4. `What Should Not Happen Next`
5. `Healthiest Next Move`
