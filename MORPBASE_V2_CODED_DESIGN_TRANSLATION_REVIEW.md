# MorpBase V2 Coded Design Translation Review

## Verdict

`Pass`

The first coded design-translation pass was worth doing.

The product still is not at its final visual identity, but it now feels more like:

- a crafted MorpBase instrument

and less like:

- a clean prototype with correct structure

## What Improved

- the shell now feels more authored and less generic
- the brand mark and top shell carry more character
- the realm surfaces now feel more like members of one designed family
- `Workspace`, `Memory`, `Community`, and `Continuity` now express their differences more clearly through atmosphere
- focus/proof surfaces feel more intentional
- the total app now feels more like one product with different modes of value

## What Still Feels Early

- the coded V2 still does not yet express the full MorpBase design universe
- the current iconography/symbol layer is still light
- public/object surfaces could still become richer later
- the product is now more distinct, but not yet fully unforgettable

## Best Next Move

The healthiest next move is:

- **do one controlled next-growth decision from the stronger base we now have**

That means:

- do not reopen the structure
- do not jump into random new features
- choose the next growth slice only after judging it against the stronger current product

So the next step should be:

- a prompt and decision for the **next controlled growth slice after the strengthened core**
