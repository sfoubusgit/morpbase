## Review Goal

Decide whether the tightening pass fixed the biggest Phase A problems:

- too much first-use density
- Preview trust being slightly too text-heavy
- keep/save emergence feeling too mechanical

## Required Judgment Criteria

Judge the tightened prototype against these plain product questions:

1. Does the Workspace now feel easier to enter?
2. Does the left side feel more guided and less like a structured form?
3. Does Preview feel more confidence-building at a glance?
4. Does the keep/save moment feel more organic?
5. Is the shell still clearly asymmetrical in the right way?
6. Is Phase A now good enough to close?

## Required Output

Write the result to:

- `MORPBASE_V2_PHASE_A_POST_TIGHTENING_GATE_REVIEW.md`

The review should contain:

1. Short executive judgment
2. What improved clearly
3. What still feels weak
4. Final gate call:
   - `Pass`
   - `Conditional Pass`
   - `Fail`
5. One recommendation for what should happen next

## Constraints

- Be plain and product-focused
- Do not hide behind technical language
- Do not keep iterating forever if the slice is good enough
- Do not promote it if the core feel is still wrong
