# MorpBase V2 Subject-Specificity Layer Prompt

Design the first light subject-specificity layer inside `Define The Subject`.

The problem to solve is:

- the subject field is now broader
- but the line can still feel too archetype-led early

## What To Decide

1. What is the lightest way to make the subject feel more particular earlier?
2. Should it be:
   - a short note
   - a few specific prompt cues
   - or a hybrid?
3. Where exactly should it sit inside Phase 1?
4. How should it avoid turning into a heavy freeform field?
5. What is the healthiest first coded slice?

## Main Protection

Do not turn this into:

- a full freewriting area
- another heavy workflow block
- or a custom prompt field pretending to be product structure

The layer should stay:

- light
- shaping-focused
- and early enough to matter

## Honest Standard

If this works, the center should gain earlier particularity without losing its calm guided rhythm.
