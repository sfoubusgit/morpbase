# MorpBase V2 Illustrator Handoff Execution Pack

## 1. Purpose

This pack exists to make the current custom asset layer usable.

It answers:

- what is already proven
- what should actually be drawn
- where each asset belongs
- what each family means
- what should stay out for now

This is not a broad brand guide.

It is:

- the first practical Illustrator handoff for MorpBase V2

## 2. What Is Already Proven

Three pilot layers now hold:

### 1. Shell pilot

What it proved:

- the top of MorpBase benefits from small authored joins
- the brand anchor benefits from one support fragment
- the shell-to-engine handoff benefits from clearer join language

### 2. Threshold pilot

What it proved:

- `Keep`, `Publish`, `Use In Workspace`, and `Activate` belong to one crossing family
- the family can be made more custom without becoming emblem-heavy

### 3. Trace pilot

What it proved:

- carried traces can feel more authored while staying quieter than thresholds
- the same trace family can hold across:
  - center arrival
  - `Memory`
  - `Community`
  - `Continuity`

## 3. Asset Families To Execute First

## 4. Geometry And Tone Rules

All first-pass assets should feel:

- fine
- restrained
- asymmetrical in a controlled way
- precise
- lightly luminous

They should not feel:

- ornamental
- ceremonial
- mystical
- luxurious for their own sake
- or logo-heavy

## 5. Family Hierarchy Rules

### Shell

- strongest whole-product identity gain
- structural first

### Thresholds

- strongest symbolic family
- should read before traces

### Traces

- quieter than thresholds
- should feel like readable residue, not symbols demanding attention

## 6. What Should Still Stay Code-Led

These should remain code-led for now:

- layout
- spacing
- hierarchy
- surface composition
- state logic
- engine flow

The custom assets should support these.
They should not replace them.

## 7. What Should Not Be Drawn Yet

Do not execute yet:

- large illustration scenes
- realm-specific art systems
- challenge or storyline imagery
- expanded card ornament
- broad wallpaper motifs
- new symbolic families

## 8. Suggested Deliverable Order

1. Shell join mini-kit
2. Brand support mini-kit
3. Threshold family mini-kit
4. Trace family mini-kit

## 9. Practical Handoff Standard

If this handoff is followed well, MorpBase should begin to feel more like:

- one authored product-world

and less like:

- a strong coded product still wearing interim marks

## 10. Honest Conclusion

The right first Illustrator execution is not:

- illustration-first
- or decoration-first

It is:

- shell
- thresholds
- traces

in exactly that order.
