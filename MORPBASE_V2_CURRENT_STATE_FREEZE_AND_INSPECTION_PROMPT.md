# MorpBase V2 Current-State Freeze And Inspection Prompt

## Goal

Inspect the current coded MorpBase V2 as one whole product and freeze the most important current judgments before any new growth.

## What This Phase Must Do

- judge the product as it currently exists
- identify what feels strongest
- identify what still feels prototype-like
- identify what feels generic or overbuilt
- identify what feels most like MorpBase
- decide what should now be protected

## Inspection Scope

Inspect:

1. `Workspace`
2. `Memory`
3. `Community`
4. `Continuity`
5. shell and product hierarchy
6. total product feeling

## Required Method

1. start from the heart of the product
2. inspect each major realm briefly
3. judge the whole product balance
4. freeze the most important current truths
5. state the likely next frontier, but do not begin it yet

## Deliverable

Write a current-state inspection doc with:

1. what currently feels strongest
2. what still feels too prototype-like
3. what currently feels most like MorpBase
4. what should now be frozen and protected
5. what the likely next frontier is after the freeze

Tone:

- plain
- honest
- product-focused
