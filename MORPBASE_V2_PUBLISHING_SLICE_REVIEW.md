# MorpBase V2 Publishing Slice Review

## Verdict

`Pass`

## What Now Works

- `Community` now feels more complete as a public loop
- the relationship between private value and public life is easier to understand
- Publishing reads as a calm circulation desk rather than a control panel
- release and withdrawal now feel like product actions around real objects, not abstract status changes

The strongest success is:

- the public side now has a more believable full shape:
  - Discover
  - creator grounding
  - Publishing

That matters because:

- the public loop feels more complete
- sharability is more legible
- and later collaboration or challenge systems now have a healthier base to attach to

## Remaining Caution

- Publishing should stay lighter than a full management system
- Community must still resist dashboard drift
- the next growth step should not inflate Publishing too quickly
- the product should still protect object meaning over public mechanics

## Best Next Move

The healthiest next move is:

- a broader whole-V2 checkpoint
- then decide whether the next controlled growth should return to:
  - private-side strengthening
  - public vitality
  - or the first truly collaborative bridge

The most important thing to protect now is:

- V2 has become stronger by completing loops carefully
- not by adding feature islands
