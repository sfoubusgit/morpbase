# Neon Yokai — 20-Prompt Set (Z-Image-Turbo, tightened)

Tighter, front-loaded prompts (subject first, fewer clauses, one short style
tail) for better Turbo adherence. Mix of portraits, wides, atmosphere, a macro
detail, a still life and a group shot.

**Shared negative:** `text, watermark, signature, lowres, blurry, extra fingers,
deformed hands, daytime, flat lighting, washed out, oversaturated`

**Settings:** Turbo → ~8 steps, CFG ~1.0–1.5. Wides 1248×832, portraits
832×1248.

**Style tail (kept on every prompt for coherence):** *neon-noir anime,
rain-slick reflections, magenta and cyan neon glow, cinematic night,
teal-and-magenta grade.*

---

01 — Hero / Scramble Crossing (wide)
```
Wide overhead establishing shot of a rain-slick pedestrian scramble crossing at 3am, nearly empty, towering walls of neon signage in magenta and cyan, wet asphalt mirroring the glow, a few scattered umbrellas and one lone still figure. Fine drizzle and volumetric haze, anamorphic flares. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, teal-and-magenta grade.
```

02 — The Konbini Ghost
```
A pale translucent ghost girl behind a convenience-store counter at night, long damp black hair over her face, hollow tired eyes, a striped konbini apron uniform. Bright shelves and a glowing drinks cooler, the dark neon street beyond the window. Flat cold fluorescent light. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, eerie.
```

03 — The Subway Kitsune
```
A fox-spirit woman on an empty late-night subway train, fox ears and white-tipped tails half-hidden under a long dark coat, golden slit-pupil eyes, a knowing smile, holding a briefcase. Cold fluorescent carriage, neon streaking past the dark windows. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, teal-and-magenta grade.
```

04 — The Neon Oni
```
Low-angle shot of a towering crimson-red oni bouncer outside a basement neon club, thick horns wrapped in glowing cyan and pink LED, gold half-lidded eyes, a tight black suit, arms folded. Hard coloured rim light from the club signage, deep shadow. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, imposing.
```

05 — The Umbrella Spirit
```
A small one-eyed umbrella spirit in a girl's shape at a rainy crossing, a single big round eye and a long playful tongue, fused with a transparent vinyl umbrella glowing with reflected neon, hopping one-legged, short rain-wet dark hair. Puddles full of pink and cyan light. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, rain-slick, playful.
```

06 — The Long-Necked Clerk
```
A rokurokubi office-worker yokai after midnight, her neck stretched unnaturally long and curving across an empty cubicle floor, black hair in a loosening bun, tired eyes behind thin glasses, a grey blouse and pencil skirt. Cold flickering office fluorescents, the neon city beyond the windows. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, uncanny.
```

07 — The Tengu Courier
```
A tengu courier riding a glowing delivery bike at speed through rain-slick neon streets, a long red nose-mask, dark feathered wings folded tight, a glowing delivery box on the back, leaning low. Signage smearing past in motion blur, wet road reflections. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, kinetic, teal-and-magenta grade.
```

08 — The Nekomata Barista
```
A nekomata cat-spirit barista at a cramped yokocho coffee stand, two cat ears and a forked tail, slit cat eyes, a sly smile, a dark apron, mid-pour. Red paper lanterns and warm amber light, cool neon haze beyond. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, warm-versus-neon contrast.
```

09 — The Yuki-onna
```
A yuki-onna snow-woman on a rain-slick neon street on the coldest night, pale flawless skin, long black hair, visible breath fogging the neon air, a pale kimono, thin frost spreading across the glowing pavement around her bare feet. Cool blue-cyan neon light and a faint cold self-glow. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, chilling and beautiful.
```

10 — The Jorogumo
```
A spider-yokai host in a silk-smooth dress in a dim host club, beautiful and composed, several extra slender limbs half-seen in the shadows behind her, a smile that has already decided your evening, faint web strands catching the light. Low warm-and-magenta club lighting. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, sultry and unsettling.
```

11 — The Bakeneko Idol
```
A bakeneko cat-spirit idol mid-performance on a small rooftop stage, cat ears and tail, a holographic idol costume with light-reactive sequins shattering neon into rainbow, fingerless gloves, a bright grin. ```

12 — The House Spirit (Zashiki-warashi)
```
A small child house-spirit in a dim capsule-hotel corridor at night, an old-fashioned red kimono, bobbed black hair, big dark eyes, a faint mischievous smile, slightly translucent. Rows of glowing pod openings in cool blue light receding behind. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, eerie and quiet.
```

13 — The Faceless One (Nopperabo)
```
A faceless yokai in a dim neon alley turning toward the viewer to reveal a smooth blank featureless face, dressed as an ordinary salaryman in a grey suit, one hand half-raised. Wet pavement, magenta and cyan signage glow, deep shadow. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, unsettling.

14 — Yokocho Lantern Alley (atmosphere)
```
A tight yokocho alley at night lined with tiny bars, rows of red paper lanterns glowing warm amber overhead, handwritten signs and noren curtains, steam rising from a yakitori grill, wet cobblestones reflecting the light, barely two people wide. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, warm and atmospheric.

15 — Neon Torii Shrine Alley (atmosphere)
```
A narrow backstreet shrine at night, a small old torii rebuilt in glowing neon tube humming electric red, stone fox statues, glowing vending machines casting cold light, tangled overhead wires, incense smoke against the neon. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, old-and-new, atmospheric.
```

16 — Rooftop Over the Sprawl (wide)
```
Wide melancholic shot of a lone spectral figure lit faintly from within, standing at a rooftop edge over an endless neon city sprawl at night, distant skyscrapers and signage to the horizon, an orange-pink light-pollution haze. The small figure dwarfed by the city. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, lonely and beautiful.
```

17 — Ramen Stall Under the Bridge
```
A tiny ramen stall under a railway bridge at night, steam rising from the hotplate, a glowing paper sign, a lone seated figure at the counter, lit warm from below and by passing train light above, wet ground reflecting neon. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, warm and intimate.
```

18 — Macro Detail / Fox Mask
```
Close-up of a white kitsune fox festival mask edged in glowing neon line, pushed to the side of a head, the painted grin lit faintly from within, the lacquered surface catching magenta and cyan city light, dark eyeholes. Shallow focus, rainy bokeh behind. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, detailed.
```

19 — Still Life / The Offering
```
A small ceramic sake cup left as an offering on a stone ledge at night, the liquid catching pink and cyan neon, a single coin and a folded paper note beside it, wet stone, a glowing vending machine out of focus behind. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, quiet and uncanny, detailed.
```

20 — Festival Crowd (group, wide)
```
A lively night street festival where yokai and humans mingle unnoticed — a fox-masked figure, a glowing umbrella spirit and a tall horned silhouette among the crowd, red lanterns and neon signage overhead, falling light rain. Neon-noir anime, cinematic night, festive with an uncanny undertow, teal-and-magenta grade.
