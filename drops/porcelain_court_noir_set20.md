# Porcelain Court × Noir Photograph — 20-Prompt Set (Z-Image-Turbo)

A Style Lab remix: the Porcelain Court universe rendered in the `style_lab_noir_photo`
look — stark black-and-white, dramatic chiaroscuro, the dolls made photographic
and all the more uncanny.

**Shared negative:** `text, watermark, signature, lowres, blurry, extra fingers,
deformed hands, color, saturated, modern clothing, cartoon, 3d render`

**Settings:** Turbo → ~8 steps, CFG ~1.0–1.5. Wides 1248×832, portraits 832×1248.

**Style tail (kept on every prompt):** *high-contrast black-and-white noir
photograph, dramatic chiaroscuro, hard key light and deep shadow, film grain,
moody and cinematic.*
