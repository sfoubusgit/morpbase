# Porcelain Court — 20-Prompt Set (Z-Image-Turbo, tightened)

Tighter, front-loaded prompts (subject first, fewer clauses, one short style
tail) for better Turbo adherence. Mix of portraits, wides, a macro detail, a
still life and a group shot.

**Shared negative:** `text, watermark, signature, lowres, blurry, extra fingers,
deformed hands, modern clothing, bright saturated colors, plastic, smooth skin`

**Settings:** Turbo → ~8 steps, CFG ~1.0–1.5. Wides 1248×832, portraits
832×1248.

**Style tail (kept on every prompt for coherence):** *rococo oil painting,
glazed porcelain skin with fine hairline cracks, dusty pastel-and-grey palette,
eerie and elegant.*

---

01 — Hero / Dust Ballroom (wide)
```
Wide establishing shot of a grand rococo ballroom long abandoned. Living porcelain dolls in faded court gowns stand frozen mid-dance across a dust-covered parquet floor, a huge dead chandelier overhead. Pale cold daylight through tall grimy windows, dust drifting in the beams. Rococo oil painting, glazed cracked porcelain skin, dusty pastel-and-grey palette, eerie and elegant.
```

02 — The Porcelain Queen
```
Regal portrait of a porcelain doll queen on a faded gilt throne. Flawless glazed bisque skin with a single hairline crack down one cheek, pale-blue glass eyes, a towering powdered silver wig and a chipped gilt crown. Dim warm light off tarnished gold, deep shadow. Rococo oil painting, dusty pastel-and-grey palette, eerie and elegant.
```

03 — The Clockwork Prince
```
Medium shot of a wind-up aristocrat doll frozen mid-bow. Pale porcelain skin with painted cheek-blush, green glass eyes, a large brass key turning in his back, an embroidered rococo frock coat with lace cuffs. Soft pale window light in a decaying gilt ballroom. Rococo oil painting, fine porcelain cracks, dusty pastel-and-grey palette, eerie and elegant.
```

04 — The Cracked Ballerina
```
A music-box ballerina doll frozen en pointe in an arabesque, one arm shattered hollow at the elbow. Cream porcelain skin with fine crazing, downcast glass eyes, a grey-cream tulle tutu. A single low warm footlight, dark velvet surround. Rococo oil painting, dusty pastel palette, eerie and elegant.
```

05 — The Masked Courtier
```
A masked doll courtier in a long gallery of foxed antique mirrors. Glossy pale porcelain skin behind a painted porcelain half-mask on a handle, a black lace fan half-raised, powdered grey hair with a black feather, a dove-grey rococo gown. Pale grey diffuse light, reflections receding into spotted infinity. Rococo oil painting, dusty pastel-and-grey palette, eerie and elegant.
```

06 — The Weeping Countess
```
Sorrowful close portrait of a mourning doll. Pale grey-toned porcelain skin finely cracked around the eyes, twin glossy glazed tear-tracks down the cheeks, a black lace mourning veil over dark hair, a black silk gown with jet beads. Soft cold window light through gauze, in a faded boudoir. Rococo oil painting, dusty pastel-and-grey palette, mournful and elegant.
```

07 — The Marionette Jester
```
An eerie marionette jester doll dangling slack on taut strings that rise to a cracked wooden control bar above. A white-and-rouge painted jester face with a cracked grin, mismatched glass eyes, a drooping belled cap, a faded rose-and-blue harlequin motley. Dim dusty light in a vast empty ballroom. Rococo oil painting, fine porcelain cracks, dusty pastel-and-grey palette, eerie.
```

08 — The Little Heir
```
A small child doll alone in an abandoned nursery, clutching a smaller worn doll. Smooth pink-tinged porcelain skin with a tiny crack at the temple, large placid blue glass eyes, a powder-blue rococo court suit with a lace collar. A dusty rocking horse and shelved dolls behind, pale grimy daylight. Rococo oil painting, dusty pastel-and-grey palette, tender and uncanny.
```

09 — The Harpsichordist
```
A gaunt musician doll at a harpsichord, long porcelain fingers locked on yellowed keys, one fingertip chipped. Ivory porcelain skin over sharp cheekbones, half-closed rapt glass eyes, an enormous dusty white wig, a wine-coloured brocade coat. Warm guttering candelabra light, deep shadow, a decaying music chamber. Rococo oil painting, dusty pastel-and-grey palette, candlelit and eerie.
```

10 — The Dollmaker
```
A severe dollmaker, part doll herself, porcelain skin patched with kintsugi-gold repair seams, sharp grey glass eyes behind half-moon spectacles, iron-grey hair in a tight bun, a charcoal work-gown and stained apron, holding a wire-threaded needle. In a cluttered atelier of porcelain limbs and blank heads, a small kiln glowing. Rococo oil painting, dusty pastel-and-grey palette, tense chiaroscuro.
```

11 — The Hollow Twins
```
Two identical porcelain dolls side by side at the foot of a grand staircase, heads tilted at the same angle. Matching pale bisque skin with the same hairline crack on each left cheek, violet glass eyes, dark ringlets with rose ribbons, identical cream lace dresses. Pale diffuse light. Rococo oil painting, fine porcelain cracks, dusty pastel-and-grey palette, uncanny and elegant.
```

12 — The Garden Statue
```
A weathered porcelain doll half-claimed by an overgrown rococo garden, green moss in her deep crazing, ivy through a shattered shoulder, clouded glass eyes lifted to the sky, gilt-blonde hair tangled with vines, a moss-streaked grey-green gown. Among collapsing topiary and cracked balustrades, soft overcast light. Rococo oil painting, dusty muted-green palette, melancholy.
```

13 — The Tea-Service Maid
```
A small servant doll frozen mid-pour over a tilted teapot, head tilted at an unnatural angle. Pale bisque skin with a chipped ear, round wide brown glass eyes, a starched white apron over a grey doll-maid dress and lace cap. A long banquet table set for a feast no one attends, dust on every plate. Rococo oil painting, dusty pastel-and-grey palette, eerie and elegant.
```

14 — Macro Detail / Cracked Face
```
Extreme close-up of a porcelain doll's face. A single large glass eye with painted lashes, a hairline crack threading across the glazed cheek, a chipped cheekbone, fine crazing in the glaze. Soft dim light, shallow focus. Rococo oil painting, dusty pastel-and-grey palette, uncanny, highly detailed.
```

15 — The Wax-Flower Conservatory
```
A porcelain doll standing among wax and silk flowers under dusty glass bell jars in a rococo conservatory, the real plants long dead and brown. Pale cracked porcelain skin, glass eyes, a faded gown. Flat grey light through grimy glass panes. Rococo oil painting, dusty pastel-and-grey palette, airless and eerie.
```

16 — The Clock-Tower Interior
```
A lone porcelain doll dwarfed among enormous brass clock-tower gears halted mid-tick, dust on the cogs, a huge cracked clock face glowing pale grey behind. Cracked porcelain skin, glass eyes, a faded court gown. A single dim shaft of light through the dial. Rococo oil painting, dusty pastel-and-grey palette, monumental and still.
```

17 — The Crypt of Discarded Dolls
```
A dim vaulted cellar catacomb of broken porcelain dolls heaped gently on stone shelves, countless glass eyes catching a single candle flame. Cracked glaze, shattered limbs, cobwebs and cold stone. Warm candlelight falling off into deep shadow. Rococo oil painting, dusty pastel-and-grey palette, macabre and tender.
```

18 — The Grand Staircase (wide)
```
Wide shot of a lone porcelain doll descending a sweeping rococo double staircase under a vast dead chandelier, threadbare red carpet and cobwebbed gilt banisters, ancestral portraits climbing the wall. Cracked glaze, glass eyes, a trailing court gown. A pale shaft of dusty light from above. Rococo oil painting, dusty pastel-and-grey palette, grand and forsaken.
```

19 — Still Life / Music Box
```
Still life of an open ornate gilt music box, a tiny ballerina turning on a mirrored stage under a glass dome, beside a tarnished porcelain tea service furred with dust and a dried rose under a bell jar. Warm candlelight, deep shadow. Rococo oil painting, dusty pastel-and-grey palette, melancholy, highly detailed.
```

20 — The Whole Court (group, wide)
```
Wide group shot of the whole porcelain doll court assembled in a faded gilt throne room — a doll queen on the throne flanked by courtiers, a jester and a ballerina, all glazed bisque skin and glass eyes, frozen and watching the viewer. Dusty shafts of pale light, a great dead chandelier above. Rococo oil painting, fine porcelain cracks, dusty pastel-and-grey palette, eerie and elegant.
```
