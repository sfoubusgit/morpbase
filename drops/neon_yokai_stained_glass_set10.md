# Neon Yokai × Stained-Glass Neon (Painterly) — 10-Prompt Set (Z-Image-Turbo)

Yokai of the rain-slick megacity rendered as **stained-glass saints of urban Japan**. Each yokai's signature palette becomes the jewel-tone panes; surrounding panes carry modern urban motifs (torii arches, lanterns, vending machines, neon signs, power lines) instead of traditional cathedral iconography. Cross-cultural devotional reframe — Japanese spirits in European glass.

---

**Recipe — Stained-Glass Neon (Painterly) (Z-Image-Turbo)**
- **Model:** Z-Image-Turbo
- **Settings:** ~8 steps, CFG 1.0–1.5
- **Resolution:** 1024×1536 portrait for yokai panels, 1536×1024 landscape for the wide rose-window
