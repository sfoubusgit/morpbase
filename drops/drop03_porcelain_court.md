## HERO — The Dust Ballroom

> A cinematic wide establishing shot of a grand rococo ballroom where the
> dancing stopped long ago. A vast parquet floor under a soft grey blanket of
> dust, towering windows and a flaking gilt mirrored wall doubling the
> emptiness, a great dead chandelier hanging low with half its crystals fallen;
> living porcelain dolls in faded court finery stand frozen mid-step across the
> floor, glazed bisque skin and glass eyes. Wide lens, deep symmetrical
> perspective down the hall. Pale cold daylight through tall grimy windows, soft
> and shadowless, dust drifting in the beams. Fine detail on cracked glaze, gilt
> and dust, muted pastel-and-grey color grade.

## 01 — The Porcelain Queen

> A regal portrait, medium shot. A towering porcelain doll queen with flawless

> pale-blue glass eyes, a small painted rosebud mouth in a serene too-still
> smile, a towering powdered silver pompadour with tarnished pearls and dried
> roses, a chipped gilt crown. Seated on a faded gilt rococo throne on a worn
> dais. Slightly low angle to stress her authority, shallow focus. Dim amber
> light bounced off tarnished gold, deep shadow around. Fine detail on the crack
> and glaze, baroque chiaroscuro, pastel-and-gilt grade.

## 03 — The Cracked Ballerina

> An intimate shot, medium. A music-box ballerina doll with smooth cream
> porcelain skin and fine crazing, one arm shattered clean and hollow at the
