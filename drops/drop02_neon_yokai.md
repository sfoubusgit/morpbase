## HERO — Scramble Crossing at 3am

> A cinematic wide establishing shot looking down over a vast pedestrian
> scramble crossing at 3am, rain-slick and nearly empty. Towering walls of neon
> signage and giant video screens in magenta, cyan and electric blue rise on
> every side; wet black asphalt mirrors the full glow above; a fine drizzle
> hazes the air, a few scattered umbrellas on the painted lines, one lone still
> figure the sparse crowd flows around. High overhead angle, deep perspective
> down into the glowing intersection. Saturated neon light with no neutral fill,
> volumetric mist threaded with colour, anamorphic flares. Wet reflective detail
> everywhere, teal shadows and magenta highlights, moody neon-noir grade.
