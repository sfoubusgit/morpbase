# Saint Circuit × Cinematic Stained-Glass Neon — 10-Prompt Set (Z-Image-Turbo)

The first run of the new **Cinematic Stained-Glass Neon** style. Where the painterly variant reads as *looking at* a stained-glass window, the cinematic variant reads as *standing in a cathedral with the stained-glass light pouring over the scene*. Saint Circuit is the perfect universe for it — every interior already wants this lighting.

---

**Recipe — Cinematic Stained-Glass Neon (Z-Image-Turbo)**
- **Model:** Z-Image-Turbo
- **Settings:** ~8 steps, CFG 1.0–1.5
- **Resolution:** 1024×1536 portrait for character panels, 1536×1024 landscape for wide scenes
- **Style tail (every prompt):** *a cinematic film-still framing, dramatic volumetric god-rays of cobalt-and-cyan stained-glass light cutting down into the dim cathedral interior, dust motes and incense haze drifting in the colored beams, deep cinematic depth of field, theatrical chiaroscuro, jewel-tone palette dominated by cobalt with magenta and gold accents, soft film-grain rendering*

**Shared negative:**
```
text, watermark, signature, lowres, blurry, deformed hands, extra fingers, flat lighting, cel shading, vector, polished digital, 3d render, plastic, daytime exterior, overexposed, photoreal photograph
```
