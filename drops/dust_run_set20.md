# Dust Run — 20-Prompt Set (Z-Image-Turbo, tightened)

Tighter, front-loaded prompts (subject first, fewer clauses, one short style
tail) for better Turbo adherence. Mix of portraits, wides, atmosphere, a macro
detail, a still life and a group shot.

**Shared negative:** `text, watermark, signature, lowres, blurry, extra fingers,
deformed hands, lush greenery, modern city, clean, oversaturated`

**Settings:** Turbo → ~8 steps, CFG ~1.0–1.5. Wides 1248×832, portraits
832×1248.

**Style tail (kept on every prompt for coherence):** *spaghetti-western
post-apocalyptic, sun-bleached tan-ochre-rust palette, dust in the air,
cinematic grit, 35mm grain.*

---

01 — Hero / High-Noon Standoff (wide)
```
Wide cinematic establishing shot down the main street of a sun-bleached ghost town at high noon, warped clapboard storefronts, a broken swinging sign, drifting sand and a tumbleweed, a lone poncho-clad gunslinger tiny down the empty street. Heat-haze and blown-out sun, hard black shadows. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, sun-bleached tan-and-ochre palette, 35mm grain.
```

02 — The Drifter
```
A nameless gunslinger on a dead-town street in the heat-haze, a sun-cured stubbled face, squinting pale eyes, a thin cigarillo, a faded sand-coloured poncho, a battered wide-brim hat low over the eyes, one hand near a holstered revolver. Hard noon side-light, deep shadow under the brim. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, sun-bleached palette, 35mm grain.
```

03 — The Water Baron
```
A corpulent water baron in a dim refinery, a sweating jowled face with an oiled moustache and a gold-tooth smile, a once-fine dusty frock coat strained at the buttons, gold rings on every finger, holding a dripping glass of clean water. Dim hanging work-lamp light, rusted tanks behind. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, grimy warm palette, gritty.
```

04 — The Mechanic
```
A rig mechanic in a rust-roofed garage, a grease-streaked face, cracked welding goggles pushed up, a grimy bandana, a roll-up cigarette, an oil-stained sleeveless jumpsuit, a heavy wrench in hand. Harsh daylight through gaps in the rusted walls, sparks behind. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, sun-bleached rust palette, gritty.
```

05 — The Bounty Hunter
```
A scarred bounty hunter in a red-rock canyon pass, a long knife-scar across one cheek, dead-calm eyes, a flat-brim black hat, a long duster strung with bounty tokens and spent shells, a bandolier, a scoped rifle on the back. Hard directional canyon light, deep cool shadow. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, austere desaturated palette, 35mm grain.
```

06 — The Dust Oracle
```
A blind desert seer at dusk, milky white eyes, deeply lined dust-caked leather-brown skin, layered sun-bleached rag wrappings and a hooded shawl, bone charms and feathers, thin hands cradling carved reading-bones. Low golden light raking through hanging dust, dunes behind. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, warm amber palette, atmospheric.
```

07 — The Last Sheriff
```
An aging lawman alone on a dead-town main street at high noon, a weathered grey-stubbled face, tired steady eyes, a tarnished sheriff star on a long dust-grey duster, a sweat-stained cowboy hat, a hand resting on the gun belt. Merciless overhead sun, hard shadow under the hat. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, sun-bleached palette, 35mm grain.
```

08 — The Scavenger Kid
```
A wiry teenage scavenger climbing through a desert scrapyard of rusted car hulks, a dirt-smudged face, a gap-toothed grin, dust goggles up on sandy hair, a too-big patched coat of pockets, a bulging scrap-sack on the back. Hard daylight with warm bounce off the rust. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, sun-bleached rust palette, gritty.
```

09 — The Road Warrior
```
A war-rig driver tearing across the dunes in a riveted scrap respirator mask and goggles, a wild dust-matted mohawk, welded scrap-metal armour over bare grease-streaked arms, gripping a chain-wrapped wheel, a huge rooster-tail of dust behind. Harsh noon sun, heavy motion blur. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, sun-bleached palette, kinetic.
```

10 — The Saloon Singer
```
A torch singer of faded glamour on a worn saloon stage, smoky kohl-rimmed eyes, dark lipstick, a knowing half-smile, a frayed red satin dress, long grey-tipped gloves, a dented microphone. Hard dusty god-rays through grimy windows, smoke in the beams. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, warm smoky palette, cinematic.
```

11 — The Dust Preacher
```
A gaunt apocalyptic preacher in a ruined dead-town chapel, a hollow-cheeked face, burning fervent eyes, a dusty black frock coat and grimy clerical collar, a flat black wide-brim hat, a sun-warped bible clutched to the chest. Hard light through broken boards, dust drifting. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, austere palette, ominous.
```

12 — The Outlaw Queen
```
A swaggering outlaw leader on the dunes at golden hour, a sharp face with a scar through one brow, a dangerous grin, a long braid under a tilted hat, a dust-red leather coat over a bandolier, twin pearl-handled revolvers in a double holster, her gang blurred behind. Low golden sun, honeyed rim light. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, warm cinematic palette.
```

13 — The Dune Sea (wide)
```
A wide cinematic shot of an endless sea of rolling sand dunes to the horizon, wind-carved ridges and rippled sand, a single faint track winding over the gold, a half-buried wreck breaking the smooth dunes, a vast pale sky. Hard low sun, long shadows. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, sun-bleached gold palette, immense.
```

14 — The Storm Wall (wide)
```
A towering mountain-high wall of dust rolling in across the desert, an immense churning haboob devouring the horizon, the light gone thick and orange-brown, the sun a dim disc, a tiny vehicle fleeing before it. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, orange-brown dust palette, apocalyptic, cinematic.
```

15 — The Bone Yard (atmosphere)
```
A dry desert basin scattered with vast bleached skeletons, enormous sun-whitened ribcages and skulls half-sunk in the sand, tangled with rusted machine wrecks, cracked earth and scrub, a hard pale sky and shimmering heat. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, bone-and-tan palette, eerie and monumental.
```

16 — The Rust Garage (interior)
```
The interior of a corrugated-iron chop shop, a half-built war-rig on blocks, engine blocks hanging on chains, walls of salvaged parts and tyres, an oil-black floor, shafts of harsh daylight through gaps in the rusted walls. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, sun-bleached rust palette, gritty and cluttered.
```

17 — The Gas Station Ruin
```
The ruin of an old highway gas station at a desert crossroads, a collapsed rusted pump canopy, a faded peeling price sign, broken fuel pumps and a looted store, sand drifting through empty doorways, weeds in the cracked forecourt. Hard noon light. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, sun-bleached palette, forlorn.
```

18 — Macro Detail / Beast Skull
```
Close-up of a sun-bleached beast skull half-buried in cracked sand, long curved horns, hollow eye sockets full of grit, weathered smooth, heat shimmer behind. Shallow focus. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, bone-and-tan palette, stark, detailed.
```

19 — Still Life / The Drifter's Kit
```
A still life on a weathered wooden crate — a worn notched revolver, a dented tin canteen, a cracked brass compass and a battered cowboy hat, dust and hard noon light. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, sun-bleached tan-and-rust palette, gritty, detailed.
```

20 — The Gang (group, wide)
```
A wide group shot of an outlaw gang facing off on the dunes at golden hour, several armed figures in dusters and bandoliers spread in a loose line, hands near holsters, a scrap war-rig behind, dust drifting, long shadows. Spaghetti-western post-apocalyptic, warm cinematic palette, tense standoff.
```
