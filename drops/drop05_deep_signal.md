## HERO — The Station on the Trench Wall

> A cinematic wide establishing shot of a deep-sea research station clinging to a
> sheer black trench wall, miles down in the abyss. A cluster of lit steel
> modules and a great glass observation dome, tiny windows glowing warm against
> infinite dark water; floodlights cut thin cones into the murk, thick with
> drifting marine snow; cables and pipes anchor it to the rock, a faint
> bioluminescent glow rising from the deep below. Wide lens, the station dwarfed
> by the crushing black, deep perspective into nothing. Cold blue-green abyssal
> light and artificial floodlight against near-total darkness. Fine particulate
> haze and volumetric beams, oppressive deep-sea cosmic-horror grade.

## 01 — The Station Commander

> An atmospheric character shot, medium close-up. A gaunt hollow-eyed station
> commander, face lit cold blue by console glow, deep exhaustion in the eyes,
> grey-streaked unwashed hair pushed back, a worn jumpsuit with faded rank
> patches and an open collar, gripping a dead radio handset too tightly. On a
> flickering deep-sea command deck, dead panels and green sonar scopes behind. Eye
> level, shallow focus. Cold blue console underlight with a slow red emergency
> pulse, deep shadow. Damp skin sheened with condensation, fine detail, tense
> sci-fi-horror grade.

## 02 — The Marine Biologist

> An eerie character shot, medium. A marine biologist underlit by bioluminescent
> tank glow, an intent fascinated face with wide eyes, dark hair escaping a loose
> tie, a rolled-sleeve lab coat over a station jumpsuit and ID lanyard, a gloved
> hand pressed flat against glowing specimen glass. In a specimen lab, a wall of
> tanks pulsing cyan and violet behind her. Eye level, shallow focus, glow
> washing up over the face. Soft bioluminescent cyan-and-violet light as the only
> source, deep surrounding dark. Fine detail on glass and skin, luminous uneasy
> grade.

## 03 — The Deep Diver

> A lonely cinematic shot, wide. A figure in a heavy riveted atmospheric dive
> suit with armoured plates and corrugated hoses, a round brass-and-steel helmet
> with a single bright lamp above a fogged cracked faceplate, an umbilical tether
> trailing up into the black. Alone in the crushing abyssal dark, silt and marine
> snow drifting in the lamp beam, bubbles streaming up. Wide lens, the diver small
> against total darkness. One hard floodlamp cone in near-total black, sharp
> falloff. Fine detail on the suit and particles, isolating deep-sea grade.

## 04 — The Station Engineer

> A tense character shot, medium. A station engineer with a grease-streaked face
> lit by a clenched torch, jaw set, short hair plastered with sweat and seawater,
> a soaked jumpsuit tied at the waist over a tank top and a tool harness, gloved
> hands working a sparking junction box. In a cramped failing corridor, seawater
> weeping from a groaning riveted seam behind her. Eye level, shallow focus,
> sparks blurred. Harsh torch light and a red emergency pulse, wet reflections.
> Fine detail on grime and steel, claustrophobic grade.

## 05 — The Comms Officer

> An unsettling character shot, medium close-up. A comms officer with a pale
> drawn face lit green by a sonar scope, sleepless red-rimmed eyes too wide and
> fixed on nothing, heavy headphones clamped over the ears with white knuckles, a
> rumpled jumpsuit, a notebook of frantic transcribed symbols. Hunched at a
> hydrophone console in the dark, a waveform forming wrong shapes on the screen.
> Eye level, shallow focus. Cold green scope-glow underlight, the rest black.
> Fine detail on the face and dials, haunted sci-fi-horror grade.

## 06 — The Changed One

> A body-horror character shot, medium. A crewmember the deep is rewriting — half
> a human face, the other half blooming into translucent fins and ridges, one
> human eye and one large lidless black abyssal eye, skin going translucent and
> faintly bioluminescent with gill-slits opening at the neck, a torn jumpsuit
> splitting over new soft anatomy, a soft internal violet glow under the flesh.
> In a dim flickering station corridor. Eye level, shallow focus. Low cold light
> with the figure's own violet inner glow. Fine detail on the changing flesh,
> uncanny dread grade.

## 07 — The Anglerfish Siren

> A seductive, horrifying shot, medium. A predator shaped like a woman, pale
> translucent abyssal flesh veined with light, huge black lidless depthless eyes,
> a single soft-gold bioluminescent lure on a stalk dangling from the brow, a
> soft beckoning smile parting over rows of needle glass teeth, long webbed
> fingers and a sinuous finned lower body trailing into the black. In open black
> water, marine snow drifting. Eye level, shallow focus on the lure and smile.
> The lure-light and her own faint glow against total dark. Fine detail on
> translucent flesh and teeth, beautiful-monstrous grade.

## 08 — The Drowned Diver

> A mournful eerie shot, medium-wide. A lost diver the sea gave back, a flooded
> broken dive suit with buckled plates and torn trailing hoses, a cracked helmet
> half-full of dark water with a faint blue glow within, the body drifting
> weightless with slack limbs slowly turning, pale waterlogged hands. Adrift in a
> flooded station corridor, small pale fish circling the helmet. Slightly low
> angle, shallow focus. A dim cold glow leaking from the cracked faceplate, deep
> shadow. Fine detail on the suit and water, ghostly grade.

## 09 — The Bioluminescent Reef

> A wondrous atmospheric wide shot, no people. An alien deep-sea reef blooming in
> the abyss, towering glowing growths and drifting jellyfish pulsing soft cyan,
> rose and gold, strange anemone-like organisms breathing light, clouds of tiny
> glowing plankton hanging in the dark. A diver floodlight grazing the colour
> from one side, total black beyond. Wide lens, deep layered depth. Soft
> self-illuminating bioluminescence fading into surrounding black. Fine detail on
> tendrils and glow, serene-yet-uncanny abyssal grade.

## 10 — The Abyssal Herald

> A sublime cosmic-horror wide shot. A vast wrongness rising in the deepest dark
> — a vaguely humanoid silhouette far too large, fins and tendrils and impossible
> wrong angles, a cold halo of bioluminescent light tracing its edges,
> constellations of small pale lights where eyes might be, a dark surface that
> seems to swallow the surrounding light. A tiny diver with one lamp dwarfed
> before it for scale, in the abyssal black. Wide lens, overwhelming negative
> space. Faint cold edge-glow against near-total void. Fine detail on the near
> diver, immense eldritch-awe grade.
