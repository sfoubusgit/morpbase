# Neon Yokai × Stained-Glass Neon — 10-Prompt Set (Z-Image-Turbo)

Testing the new Stained-Glass Neon style (painterly semi-real anime, dramatic
red/blue neon gel lighting, luminous stained-glass jewel accents) on Neon Yokai.
Not yet banked in Style Lab — test first, then keep.

**Shared negative:** `text, watermark, signature, lowres, blurry, extra fingers,
deformed hands, flat lighting, dull, washed out, daytime`

**Settings:** Turbo → ~8 steps, CFG ~1.0–1.5.

**Style tail:** *painterly semi-realistic anime, dramatic complementary neon gel
lighting in red and electric blue, cool blue shadows and hot red-magenta
highlights, luminous stained-glass jewel-tone accents with black leading, high
saturation and contrast, glowing neon tubes, drifting smoke, cinematic moody
night, rich brushwork.*
