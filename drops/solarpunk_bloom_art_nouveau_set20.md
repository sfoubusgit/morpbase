# Solarpunk Bloom × Art Nouveau — 20-Prompt Set (Z-Image-Turbo)

A Style Lab remix: the Solarpunk Bloom universe rendered in the `style_lab_art_nouveau`
look. Tight, front-loaded prompts in the classic Mucha decorative-panel format
(figure + halo disk + ornate botanical border).

**Shared negative:** `text, watermark, signature, lowres, blurry, extra fingers,
deformed hands, 3d render, photograph, harsh shadows, dark gloomy palette`

**Settings:** Turbo → ~8 steps, CFG ~1.0–1.5. Panels portrait 832×1248, landscape
panels 1248×832.

**Style tail (kept on every prompt):** *Art Nouveau illustration, Alphonse Mucha
style, sinuous flowing linework, flat decorative colour with gold accents, soft
pastel-and-green palette, elegant and organic.*
