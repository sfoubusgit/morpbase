# Deep Signal — 20-Prompt Set (Z-Image-Turbo, tightened)

Tighter, front-loaded prompts (subject first, fewer clauses, one short style
tail) for better Turbo adherence. Mix of portraits, wides, atmosphere, a macro
detail, a still life and a group shot.

**Shared negative:** `text, watermark, signature, lowres, blurry, extra fingers,
deformed hands, daylight, bright cheerful, sunny, above water, oversaturated`

**Settings:** Turbo → ~8 steps, CFG ~1.0–1.5. Wides 1248×832, portraits
832×1248.

**Style tail (kept on every prompt for coherence):** *bioluminescent deep-sea
cosmic horror, abyssal black and steel, glowing cyan and violet, cinematic,
dread.*

---

01 — Hero / Station on the Trench Wall (wide)
```
Wide establishing shot of a deep-sea research station clinging to a sheer black trench wall, miles down, a cluster of lit steel modules and a glass dome, tiny warm windows against infinite dark water, floodlight cones thick with marine snow, a faint glow rising from below. The station dwarfed by crushing black. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, abyssal black and steel, cinematic, dread.
```

02 — The Station Commander
```
A gaunt hollow-eyed station commander on a flickering command deck, face lit cold blue by console glow, deep exhaustion, grey-streaked unwashed hair, a worn jumpsuit with faded rank patches, gripping a dead radio handset. Dead panels and green sonar scopes behind, a slow red emergency pulse. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, cold blue glow, dread.
```

03 — The Marine Biologist
```
A marine biologist underlit by glowing specimen tanks, an intent fascinated face, wide eyes, dark hair escaping a tie, a lab coat over a jumpsuit, a gloved hand pressed flat to glowing glass. A wall of tanks pulsing cyan and violet behind. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, cyan-and-violet glow, eerie.
```

04 — The Deep Diver
```
A lone figure in a heavy riveted atmospheric dive suit, a round brass helmet with a single bright lamp above a fogged cracked faceplate, an umbilical tether trailing up into the black, bubbles streaming, silt drifting in the beam. Alone in crushing abyssal dark. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, one floodlamp in black, isolating.
```

05 — The Station Engineer
```
A station engineer in a cramped flooding corridor, a grease-streaked face lit by a clenched torch, short hair plastered with seawater, a soaked jumpsuit and tool harness, gloved hands at a sparking junction box, seawater weeping from a groaning seam behind. Red emergency pulse, wet reflections. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, claustrophobic, dread.
```

06 — The Comms Officer
```
A comms officer hunched at a hydrophone console in the dark, a pale drawn face lit green by a sonar scope, sleepless red-rimmed eyes too wide, heavy headphones clamped on with white knuckles, a notebook of frantic symbols, a wrong waveform on the screen. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, cold green glow, haunted.
```

07 — The Station Doctor
```
A station doctor in a cramped med bay, a calm tired face with a faint translucent sheen at the jaw, hair under a surgical cap, scrubs and gloves under a stained coat, holding a chart of impossible scans, faint luminous veins under the skin. Cold clinical light, deep shadow. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, uneasy, dread.
```

08 — The Deep Cultist
```
A deep cultist in a dripping flooded chamber, a serene rapturous face, salt-cracked lips, a beatific smile, pale devoted eyes blown wide, sodden barnacle-crusted robes hung with shells and bone, grey waterlogged skin, cradling a barnacle idol. Dim cold light, water everywhere. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, eerie devotion.
```

09 — The Changed One
```
A crewmember being rewritten by the deep, half a human face and half blooming into translucent fins and ridges, one human eye and one large lidless black abyssal eye, translucent faintly glowing skin, gill-slits at the neck, a torn jumpsuit, a soft violet inner glow. A dim flickering corridor. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, body horror, violet glow.
```

10 — The Anglerfish Siren
```
A predator shaped like a woman in black water, pale translucent abyssal flesh veined with light, huge black lidless depthless eyes, a single soft-gold bioluminescent lure on a stalk from the brow, a soft beckoning smile over rows of needle glass teeth, long webbed fingers, a finned lower body trailing into the dark. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, lure-light in black, beautiful and monstrous.
```

11 — The Drowned Diver
```
A drowned diver drifting in a flooded corridor, a broken dive suit with buckled plates and torn hoses, a cracked helmet half-full of dark water with a faint blue glow within, limbs slack and slowly turning, pale waterlogged hands, small pale fish circling. A dim cold glow from the faceplate. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, ghostly, dread.
```

12 — The Abyssal Herald
```
A vast wrongness rising in the deepest dark, a vaguely humanoid silhouette far too large, fins and tendrils and impossible wrong angles, a cold halo of bioluminescent light tracing its edges, constellations of small pale lights where eyes might be, a tiny diver with one lamp dwarfed before it. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, immense, eldritch awe.
```

13 — The Observation Dome (atmosphere)
```
The interior of a deep-sea observation dome, a great curved glass viewport onto bottomless black water, drifting marine snow and faint far-off bioluminescent flickers beyond, a riveted steel frame, a single worn chair facing out, condensation on the glass. Dim cold blue glow. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, silent, dread.
```

14 — The Bioluminescent Reef (atmosphere)
```
An alien deep-sea reef blooming in the abyss, towering glowing growths and drifting jellyfish pulsing soft cyan, rose and gold, strange anemone-like organisms breathing light, clouds of tiny glowing plankton in the dark, a floodlight grazing the colour from one side. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, serene and uncanny.
```

15 — The Hydrothermal Vents (atmosphere)
```
A field of black-smoker hydrothermal vents on the trench floor, mineral chimneys belching dark superheated water, shimmering heat-haze rising through cold black, pale ghostly tube worms and blind crabs, a faint hellish red glow from the vent mouths. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, primordial, eerie.
```

16 — The Moon Pool (atmosphere)
```
A station moon pool, an open square of black seawater in the floor breathing gently upward, ripples lit cold from below, dive suits and helmets racked around it, a launch crane and chains over the dark water, rising mist on wet steel grating. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, cold glow, tense.
```

17 — The Sunken Wreck (atmosphere)
```
An older sunken wreck on the trench floor, a half-buried hull drowned long ago, draped in grey silt and pale bioluminescent growth, collapsed bulkheads and doorways gaping into black, a school of pale fish pouring from a broken porthole, a diver floodlight grazing the corroded metal. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, forsaken, dread.
```

18 — Macro Detail / Helmet Faceplate
```
Close-up of a deep-sea diving helmet faceplate, thick glass fogged with condensation and threaded with a hairline crack, a riveted brass-and-steel frame, a dim face barely visible within, cold blue light. Shallow focus. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, detailed, eerie.
```

19 — Still Life / The Lab Bench
```
A still life on a dim lab bench — a sealed glass specimen jar with a glowing cyan organism inside, a barnacle-crusted idol, and a hydrophone console glowing green, in deep shadow. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, glowing accents in black, detailed, ominous.
```

20 — The Crew (group, wide)
```
A wide group shot of the station crew gathered on a failing command deck, several figures in worn jumpsuits lit by cold blue consoles and a red emergency pulse, tense faces turned toward a wrong signal on the main screen, steam and shadow. Bioluminescent deep-sea cosmic horror, cold glow, dread.
```
