w# MorpBase V2 Coded Whole-Product Inevitability Tightening Brief

## Goal

Make the whole coded V2 feel:

- calmer
- more hierarchically inevitable
- less assembled from many equally strong support blocks

without:

- adding systems
- reducing meaning
- or flattening the product

## Main Tightening Target

The main target is:

- **secondary support-surface hierarchy**

## What Should Change

### 1. Secondary support surfaces

These should feel:

- lighter
- calmer
- more clearly subordinate to the main signature surfaces

Examples:

- smaller support notes
- trace blocks
- some secondary framing panels
- auxiliary explanatory boxes

### 2. Realm-family balance

The support layers should still carry realm temperament, but more quietly.

### 3. Whole-product reading

The total screen should feel less like:

- many good boxes

and more like:

- a few strong primary places with calmer support around them

## What Should Not Change

- no new product behavior
- no heavier shell ornament
- no bigger motif spread
- no feature growth

## Success Condition

This pass succeeds if the honest reaction becomes:

- `the product breathes better`
- `the main places matter more`
- `the whole feels less assembled`
