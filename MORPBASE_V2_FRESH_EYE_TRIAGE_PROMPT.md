# MorpBase V2 Fresh-Eye Triage Prompt

Goal:
- prepare the smallest useful decision map for interpreting the next fresh-eye reaction

What this should do:
- take the most likely reaction types
- map each one to the right next move
- prevent overreaction, underreaction, or random tuning

Guardrails:
- keep the map simple
- only open one tuning lane at a time
- if the baseline holds, prefer freezing over more motion

Success condition:
- the next fresh-eye reading can turn directly into one disciplined follow-up move
