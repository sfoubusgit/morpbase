# MorpBase V2 Workspace Feel-Tuning Review Prompt

## Goal

Judge whether the recent `Workspace` feel-tuning pass improved the center in the right way.

## What Changed

The `Workspace` now:

- shows one clearer active phase at a time
- keeps non-active phases calmer through condensed summaries
- still allows intentional re-entry through the phase rail
- shows a clearer `Current Focus`
- keeps `Prompt Preview` and `Keep` in their existing trust role

## Review Questions

1. Does the center feel less like a larger form now?
2. Does it still feel open enough to revisit?
3. Did the phase rhythm become more natural?
4. Did the feel pass avoid turning into another structure-heavy layer?

## Deliverable

Write a short review with:

- pass / conditional pass / fail
- what improved
- what still needs watching
- the healthiest next move
