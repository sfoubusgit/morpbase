# MorpBase V2 Support Realm Proportion Tightening Brief

## Intent

This step should tighten whole-product proportion now that the `Workspace` center is strong enough to pause.

## Problem

The product is no longer mainly weak in:

- center logic
- center breadth
- or center flow

The next risk is:

- support realms reading too evenly beside a stronger center

## Scope

Design only:

- the next restrained proportion-tightening move across `Memory`, `Community`, and `Continuity`

Do not design:

- more realm features
- more center growth
- more public participation growth
- more continuity growth

## Main Goal

Keep the support realms:

- real
- useful
- integrated

But make them:

- calmer
- more proportionate
- more clearly secondary to the center

## Honest Standard

If this works, the next coded pass should make the total product feel more like:

- one strong creative engine with support layers

instead of:

- several similarly forceful product zones
