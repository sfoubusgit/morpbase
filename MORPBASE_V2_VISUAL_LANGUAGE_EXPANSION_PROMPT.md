# MorpBase V2 Visual Language Expansion Prompt

Design the first controlled expansion of `Shape The Look` inside the V2 `Workspace`.

The goal is:

- make Phase 2 feel richer
- without turning it into a style library
- and without making the center heavier

Focus on:

- the visual-direction field first
- then how mood should relate to it

Judge:

- how many additional look directions are healthy
- whether they should be grouped
- whether mood should stay broad or become slightly more specific

Avoid:

- giant taxonomies
- trend chasing
- model-site style lists
- aesthetic chaos

Prefer:

- a curated field
- calm grouping if needed
- product-real legibility
