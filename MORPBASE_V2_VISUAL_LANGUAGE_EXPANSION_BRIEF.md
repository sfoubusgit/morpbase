# MorpBase V2 Visual Language Expansion Brief

## Intent

This step should make `Shape The Look` feel less prototype-thin.

## Problem

The subject side is now stronger because it has:

- broader entry
- earlier subject particularity

So the next narrowness in the center is now more visible in Phase 2.

## Scope

Design only:

- the first controlled expansion of `Visual Direction`
- and, if needed, a small adjustment to `Mood And Light`

Do not design:

- a large style library
- new workflow phases
- more subject growth
- edge-system growth

## Main Goal

Keep Phase 2:

- curated
- calm
- readable

But make it:

- richer
- more believable
- and more balanced against the stronger Phase 1

## Honest Standard

If this works, the next coded center slice should make `Shape The Look` feel:

- more expressive
- but still guided

instead of:

- obviously narrower than the subject side
