# MorpBase V2 Threshold Custom Asset Pilot Review

## Verdict

- **Pass**

## What Improved

The threshold family now feels more like:

- one authored crossing language

and less like:

- four separate good threshold treatments

### 1. The bands now feel more family-held

The new corner and lower-line fragments help the threshold bands feel:

- more deliberately framed
- more related
- and more custom

without making them feel:

- emblem-heavy
- or ceremonial

### 2. `Keep` now belongs more clearly to the same action family

This mattered.

Before, `Publish`, `Use`, and `Activate` already carried a stronger action-mark reading.

Now `Keep` joins that family more clearly, so the four core crossings feel more evenly authored.

### 3. The family stayed restrained

The pilot did not turn thresholds into:

- mini logos
- reward marks
- or decorative trophies

They still read first as:

- meaningful crossings

## What This Pass Did Not Break

Important:

- the threshold family still stays smaller than the product itself
- the crossings still feel calm
- `Keep`, `Publish`, `Use`, and `Activate` still keep different meanings inside one family

## Recommended Next Move

- **trace custom asset pilot**

Why:

- shell now has a first custom bridge
- thresholds now have a first custom bridge
- and traces are the next symbolic family that can benefit from the same treatment

## Honest Conclusion

This was the right second custom asset pilot because it strengthened one of the most MorpBase-native symbolic families without pushing the product into visual theater.
