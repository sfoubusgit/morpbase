# MorpBase V2 Creator Grounding Slice Review

## Verdict

`Pass`

## What Now Works

- `Community` feels less anonymous
- public objects now feel more clearly attached to a real practice
- the creator layer adds human context without replacing the objects
- the first creator practice panel gives Community a stronger public meaning

The strongest success is:

- the public side now feels more like creative practice and less like a shelf of public entries

That matters because it gives later systems a healthier base:

- sharability becomes more meaningful
- later collaboration has a clearer human anchor
- later challenges could grow from creator-hosted or creator-shaped participation

## Remaining Caution

- this should remain an object-grounded creator layer, not a profile-first system
- Community should still resist generic social identity inflation
- the product should not drift into stats, followers, or creator theater too early
- publishing still remains a meaningful later gap

## Best Next Move

The healthiest next move is:

- a broader checkpoint on the coded V2 public side
- then decide whether the next growth should be:
  - a calm publishing slice
  - or another bridge layer that strengthens the same object engine

The important thing to protect now is:

- Community is becoming more human without becoming more generic
