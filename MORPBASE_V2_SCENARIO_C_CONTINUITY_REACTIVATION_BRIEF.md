# MorpBase V2 Scenario C Continuity Reactivation Brief

## Scenario

`Continuity Reactivation`

## Path

1. open a continuity line
2. judge whether it already feels like readable recurrence
3. choose `Activate`
4. return into `Workspace`
5. judge whether the carried line still feels recognizably the same while active

## What to judge

- does `Continuity` still feel lighter than the other realms
- does recurrence feel readable through real work, not explanation
- does `Activate` still feel like a meaningful crossing
- does the returned line still feel like carried sameness, not just another loaded draft

## Plain questions

- does this realm still feel real without becoming heavy
- does activation feel like payoff or just relabeling
- does the returned line still feel recognizably carried
- does this still feel like one engine

## Failure signs

- the realm feels abstract or managerial
- activation feels purely explanatory
- the returned line loses its continuity feeling once active
- the path feels disconnected from the stronger center

## Success reading

If this scenario passes, the first validation round will have proven that all three major return systems can feed real creation without breaking the center.
