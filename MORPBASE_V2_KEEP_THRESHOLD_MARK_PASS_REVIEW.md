# MorpBase V2 Keep Threshold-Mark Pass Review

## Verdict

- **Pass**

## What Improved

The `Keep` surface now feels more like:

- a crossing

and less like:

- the last well-styled panel in the right column

### 1. The crossing now has a visible family shape

The new threshold band gives `Keep` a clearer sense of:

- a line moving across a boundary

That is more native to MorpBase than adding a badge or decorative icon.

### 2. The threshold language still feels restrained

This is important.

The pass did not turn into:

- ceremonial UI
- symbolic theater
- or gamified reward styling

It stayed:

- calm
- structural
- and meaning-first

### 3. `Keep` now better supports the future threshold family

This is a good bridge move because it proves the threshold family can live in the product without overwhelming it.

## What Still Is Not Final

This is still not the final Illustrator-backed threshold language.

The current pass is:

- the coded bridge

The final threshold family will still want:

- refined bracket shapes
- better carried-node treatment
- and calmer custom line fragments later

## What This Pass Did Not Break

Important:

- `Keep` did not become louder
- the panel did not become more dramatic than the rest of the product
- the action still feels product-real

## Recommended Next Move

The healthiest next move is:

- **Publish threshold-mark pass**

Why:

- `Publish` is the clearest second threshold
- it is already visible in the product
- and it will help prove the family can stretch from private crossing to public crossing

## Honest Conclusion

This was a good first threshold-family bridge because it made `Keep` feel more MorpBase-native without turning the product symbolic for its own sake.
