# MorpBase V2 Public Line Family Review

## Verdict

`Pass`

## What Now Works

- V2 now lets a public workflow result read more clearly as the center of a small related family
- the original line, the public release, and the connected inward responses can be understood together
- the product now feels less like isolated public objects and more like living related work
- publishing also reads more meaningfully because public objects now show whether a family has begun to form

The strongest success is:

- public work now feels more connected without becoming thread-heavy

That matters because it keeps MorpBase:

- object-led
- creation-centered
- and more alive without becoming socially noisy

## Remaining Caution

- this layer should stay small and readable
- it must not drift into storyline management or thread software
- connected family reading should not become more important than the public object itself
- the next step should still resist challenge-platform behavior

## Best Next Move

The healthiest next move is:

- a restrained `response direction` slice

Why:

- public work can now show invitations
- visible responses
- and a readable family

What it still lacks is:

- a calmer way to express what kind of response a creator is actually inviting

That is a stronger next frontier than jumping straight to challenges or storylines.
