# MorpBase V2 Scene Pressure Layer Brief

## Intent

This step should make `Stage The Image` feel less compressed.

## Problem

The center is now stronger in:

- subject
- early specificity
- visual language

So the next narrowness is easier to feel in `Phase 3`, which currently relies too heavily on framing alone.

## Scope

Design only:

- the first light `scene-pressure` layer inside `Phase 3`

Do not design:

- environment building
- composition software
- new workflow phases
- more subject or look growth
- edge-system growth

## Main Goal

Keep `Stage The Image`:

- calm
- guided
- compact

But make it:

- more believable
- more expressive
- and more balanced against the stronger first two phases

## Honest Standard

If this works, the next coded center slice should make `Stage The Image` feel:

- like real staging

instead of:

- just a framing pick before Keep
