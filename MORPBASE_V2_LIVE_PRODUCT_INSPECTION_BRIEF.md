# MorpBase V2 Live Product Inspection Brief

## Purpose

Inspect the current coded V2 as one product, not as a list of features.

The goal is to judge:

- what already feels convincingly MorpBase
- what still feels too prototype-like
- and where the future design-universe layer will matter most

## Main Inspection Questions

### 1. Does the center feel unquestioned?

When you enter the product:

- does `Workspace` clearly feel like where the product begins?

### 2. Does `Memory` feel like a second home?

When a line crosses out of `Workspace`:

- does it really feel like it now lives somewhere meaningful?

### 3. Does `Community` feel like public life of real work?

When you inspect a public result:

- does it feel worth opening for the right reasons?

### 4. Does `Continuity` feel real but lighter?

When you enter `Continuity`:

- does it belong?
- and does it still stay appropriately secondary?

### 5. Does the product feel too boxed?

When you move through the realms:

- does the product breathe enough?
- or does it still feel slightly assembled from many support panels?

### 6. Does the product still feel too prototype-like anywhere?

Look for places where the reaction is:

- "this is correct"

instead of:

- "this really feels like the product"

### 7. Where is the future design-universe layer most needed?

Look for the places that most want:

- stronger custom identity
- custom motif support
- more authored visual world

## What To Note During Inspection

Try to note things in plain terms like:

- too boxed
- too app-like
- promising
- feels real
- still too prototype
- Memory feels right
- Community still a bit too UI-heavy
- shell feels strong / weak

That is more useful now than technical feedback.

## Honest Goal

This inspection is not for proving the product is done.
It is for identifying:

- what is already strong enough to protect
- and what deserves the next true design-universe execution phase
