# MorpBase V2 Keep Threshold Deepening Brief

## 1. The Real Keep Gap

The current `Keep` surface is already correct.

The gap is:

- it still feels slightly too much like the last panel in the right column
- and slightly too little like a meaningful crossing into `Memory`

Plainly:

- the user understands `Keep`
- but the surface can still feel more like a threshold

## 2. What Keep Should Feel Like

`Keep` should feel like:

- the point where the line becomes worth carrying forward

That means it should make clear:

- what conditions are being crossed
- why the line is now worth preserving
- what the line becomes after crossing

The strongest reading is:

- not “save this”
- but “this line now deserves a second home”

## 3. What Should Change In This Pass

### 1. Threshold reading

The surface should more clearly show:

- whether the structural anchors are present

### 2. Crossing meaning

The surface should more clearly show:

- what the line becomes when it crosses

### 3. Carry-forward feeling

The surface should more clearly imply:

- this moves into `Memory` as living value
- not frozen output

## 4. What Should Stay Out

Do not add:

- more workflow complexity
- progress bars
- rigid checklist UI
- celebratory success styling
- heavy symbolic ritual

This should stay:

- restrained
- calm
- product-real

## 5. Success Condition

This pass succeeds if the honest reaction becomes:

- `Keep now feels more like a crossing`
- `I understand what makes the line ready`
- `Memory feels more connected to this moment`

## 6. Honest Conclusion

The strongest next inevitability move is to make `Keep` feel more like the point where a line earns its second home. That is the right way to deepen the threshold without turning it into product theater.
