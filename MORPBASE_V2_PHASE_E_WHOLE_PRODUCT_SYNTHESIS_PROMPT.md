# MorpBase V2 Phase E Whole-Product Synthesis Prompt

Use this prompt when MorpBase V2 has already proven its major prototype slices separately and now needs one stitched synthesis pass.

Your job is to determine what the final first-wave synthesis slice must prove so that MorpBase V2 can be judged as one integrated product instead of several adjacent mini-apps.

## Core Goal

Decide how to prototype the whole product in one stitched experience that tests:

- whether `Workspace` still feels like the unquestioned center
- whether `Memory` still feels like the strongest second home
- whether `Community` and `Continuity` return value back into creation instead of pulling the product apart
- whether the shell hierarchy is still felt in use
- whether future qualities like sharability, collaboration, playfulness, challenges, and storylines could attach naturally later without breaking the core

## Required Inputs

Read these first:

- `MORPBASE_V2_FOUNDATION_FREEZE.md`
- `MORPBASE_V2_PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md`
- `MORPBASE_V2_INTERACTION_BLUEPRINT.md`
- `MORPBASE_V2_PROTOTYPE_EXECUTION_WORKFLOW.md`
- `MORPBASE_V2_PROTOTYPE_PHASE_A_TO_D_CHECKPOINT_REASSESSMENT.md`
- `MORPBASE_V2_IMAGE_ROLE_ANALYSIS.md`

Also use the Phase A to D prototype learnings as evidence.

## Main Questions

Answer these directly:

1. What must the stitched Phase E journey include to test the real whole product?
2. What should be shown as actual lived transitions, not just labels in a shell?
3. What should stay out of scope because it would bloat or confuse the synthesis?
4. How can future ideas like sharing, collaboration, challenges, and storylines be acknowledged without turning the synthesis into feature inflation?
5. What is the main product question this Phase E slice must answer?

## Required Output

Write a concise but complete result that includes:

- the executive purpose of Phase E
- what it must prove
- the main stitched journey
- the minimum artifact set
- the core states that must be shown
- the critical transitions that must be felt
- what can stay mocked
- what would count as failure
- the single main product question for the slice

Save the result as:

- `MORPBASE_V2_PHASE_E_WHOLE_PRODUCT_SYNTHESIS_BRIEF.md`

## Important Rules

- Do not reopen the V2 foundation casually.
- Do not turn this into shell polish only.
- Do not invent new major realms.
- Do not let Community or Continuity overshadow the center.
- Keep images as proof of value, not the center.
- Treat future sharability, collaboration, playfulness, challenges, and storylines as attachment pressures around the same engine, not as separate worlds.

## Final Standard

The result should make it possible to build one prototype slice that can honestly answer:

- **does MorpBase V2 already feel like one integrated creative engine, or still like several strong parts living beside each other?**
