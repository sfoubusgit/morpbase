# MorpBase V2 Whole-Product Reassessment Prompt

You are analyzing **MorpBase V2** as a **completely new rebuilt product**, not as a refinement of V1.

Your task is to perform a serious reassessment of the **entire current V2 product picture**.

This is not another local realm decision.

You must test whether the current V2 foundation still holds when considered as one product:

- `Workspace`
- `Memory`
- `Continuity`
- `Community`
- the long-range horizon above them

The major pieces currently established include:

- a workflow-centered product
- `Workspace` as the dominant center
- `Memory` as the strong second pole
- `Continuity` as a lighter but real realm
- `Community` as a public circulation layer
- shared creative object flow
- benchmark workflow and workspace structure
- public object model and Community sub-surfaces

You must assess whether these decisions now form:

- one coherent product
- with the right hierarchy
- the right asymmetry
- the right amount of ambition
- and the right resistance to V1-style drift

## Critical Constraints

- Treat V2 as a **fresh rebuild**
- Do **not** assume earlier decisions are correct just because they already exist
- Question whether the total shape is actually product-strong
- Look for hidden contradictions, over-complexity, weak handoffs, equal-center drift, or over-theorized structure
- Do **not** protect earlier decisions if they fail at whole-product level

## Method

### 1. Start with real-life whole-product journeys first

Write at least 5 realistic V2 product journeys such as:

- first-time user creates a first result and keeps it
- returning user branches from Memory into a new workflow
- creator publishes work into Community and later reuses something public
- continuity-aware user works with recurring entities across multiple sessions
- later-growth user touches multiple layers without the product feeling fragmented

For each journey, explain what system-level demands it creates.

### 2. Re-state the current total V2 picture

Summarize the current product as one system, not as separate docs.

### 3. Identify the biggest tension points

Look for things like:

- too many visible realms
- weak first-use hierarchy
- weak handoff between Workspace and Memory
- Continuity too weak or too abstract
- Community too rich or too detached
- the long-range horizon contaminating first-wave decisions
- too much conceptual layering vs practical product strength

### 4. Compare at least 3 whole-product readings

For example:

- the current asymmetric layered product
- a more center-heavy simplified product
- a broader platform-style product

You may define stronger alternatives if needed.

### 5. Choose the strongest final reading

Decide whether:

- the current direction holds
- it needs correction
- or it needs recomposition

### 6. Make the result concrete

Define:

- what MorpBase V2 fundamentally is now
- what the strongest hierarchy really is
- what the biggest guardrails are
- what the biggest unresolved gaps still are
- what the next best structural step should be

## Output Requirements

Write the result as:

`MORPBASE_V2_WHOLE_PRODUCT_REASSESSMENT.md`

Structure it with:

1. Executive Conclusion
2. Real-Life Whole-Product Journeys
3. Current Total V2 Product Picture
4. Major Tensions And Risks
5. Candidate Whole-Product Readings
6. Chosen Final Reading
7. Guardrails
8. Remaining Gaps
9. Recommended Next Step
10. Final Judgment

The result should be reflective, corrective where needed, and willing to simplify or sharpen the total V2 picture if the current structure does not fully hold.
