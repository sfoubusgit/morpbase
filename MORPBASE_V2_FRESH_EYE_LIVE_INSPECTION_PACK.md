# MorpBase V2 Fresh-Eye Live Inspection Pack

Use this while looking at the live V2.

## Main question

Does this feel like a real MorpBase product now, or still like a very well-structured prototype?

## Center

- Does `Workspace` clearly feel like the heart of the product?
- Does the center feel guided and alive, or still a little too arranged?
- Does `Prompt Preview` feel trust-building or merely informative?

## Memory

- Does `Memory` feel like a second home for living work?
- Or does it still feel a little too organized / archive-like?

## Community

- Does `Community` feel like the public life of real MorpBase work?
- Or does it still feel a little too much like a content surface?

## Continuity

- Does `Continuity` feel light but meaningful?
- Or does it still feel a little too explained?

## Whole Product

- Do all parts still feel like one engine?
- Does anything feel too equal in weight against the center?
- Does anything feel too neat, too app-like, or too generic?

## Final read

If one thing feels clearly off, write it as simply as possible:

- `too generic`
- `too neat`
- `too app-like`
- `memory still too archive-like`
- `community still too content-like`
- `continuity still too abstract`
- `workspace still not strong enough`

If nothing feels clearly off, that is also useful:

- `the current baseline holds`
