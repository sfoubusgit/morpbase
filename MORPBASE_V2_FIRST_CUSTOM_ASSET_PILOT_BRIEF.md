# MorpBase V2 First Custom Asset Pilot Brief

## Intent

This pilot should prove that MorpBase can begin to wear a truly custom visual layer without losing:

- clarity
- hierarchy
- restraint

## Pilot Scope

The first pilot should stay narrow.

Use only:

- shell joins and top-frame markers
- one refined brand-anchor support fragment
- one engine-strip join family

Do not include yet:

- big illustrations
- realm art systems
- broad trace fragments
- card-level decorative assets

## What The Pilot Must Achieve

The pilot should make the product feel more:

- authored
- singular
- product-real

without making it feel more:

- ornate
- premium-for-its-own-sake
- or theatrically branded

## Suggested First Deliverables

### 1. Shell join strip

- top-frame divider idea
- shell-to-engine join idea
- one calmer separator logic

### 2. Brand anchor support

- a refined micro-mark or mark fragment
- a clearer mark-to-frame relationship

### 3. Realm-weight support

- one subtle structural cue that strengthens:
  - `Workspace`
  - `Memory`
  - `Community`
  - `Continuity`

without turning them into four visual sub-brands

## Honest Standard

If this pilot works, MorpBase should feel more like:

- one authored world

and less like:

- a strong coded product still waiting for its real skin
