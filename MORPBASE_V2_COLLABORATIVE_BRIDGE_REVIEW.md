# MorpBase V2 Collaborative Bridge Review

## Verdict

`Pass`

## What Now Works

- V2 now shows the first believable bridge from public work to shared continuation
- collaboration begins through a real object, not through social presence
- the continuation path still routes inward through `Memory` and back toward `Workspace`
- the slice adds a new human frontier without creating a second product center

The strongest success is:

- collaboration now starts to mean "continue this work" instead of "enter a social space"

That is very important for MorpBase because it keeps the product:

- workflow-centered
- object-centered
- and meaning-first

## Remaining Caution

- this bridge should stay narrower than full collaboration
- it must not drift into chat, shared editing, or generic social tooling too quickly
- the public side should still protect object meaning over collaboration mechanics
- the next step should not assume that broader collaboration is automatically ready

## Best Next Move

The healthiest next move is:

- another broader whole-V2 checkpoint

Why:

- the product has now crossed an important boundary
- it has moved from private/public loops into the first shared-continuation behavior
- that makes this exactly the right moment to step back again and judge the total shape before expanding further

The main thing to protect now is:

- MorpBase is strongest when every new capability still returns to real creation
