# CivitAI Account Launch Kit — Phase 0

Everything needed to brand the account and start publishing the drops in
`drops/`. Companion to MARKETING_CAMPAIGN.md.

---

## 1. Persona & handle
A **worldbuilder/curator persona** with MorpBase woven into the bio (not the
name) reads as a creator to follow rather than an ad.

- **Recommended:** *Atlas of Made Worlds* — handle `@AtlasOfMadeWorlds`
  (fallbacks: `@MadeWorldsAtlas`, `@AtlasOfWorlds`)
- **Brand-forward alt:** *MorpBase Worlds* — `@MorpBaseWorlds`
- **Craftsman alt:** *The Worldsmith* — `@TheWorldsmith`

**Voice:** confident worldbuilder/curator. Lore-forward, restrained, no
"🔥 masterpiece 8k" hype. Let the worlds talk.

## 2. Bio
- **Primary (≈120 chars):** *"I build one coherent world a week — same cast,
  places, and look — then share every prompt. Made in MorpBase ↓"*
- **Short alt:** *"Coherent worlds, one a week. All prompts shared. Made in
  MorpBase ↓"*
- **Link:** your MorpBase URL.

## 3. Avatar + banner
- **Avatar (constant forever, for recognition):** a single iconic emblem on a
  deep neutral background — e.g. a stylised faceted globe / compass-of-worlds.
  Must read at small size. Don't change it between drops.
- **Banner:** a horizontal strip of 3–4 world **hero images** side by side
  (Neon Yokai | Solarpunk Bloom | Porcelain Court | Dust Run) under a thin title
  bar: *"Atlas of Made Worlds — a new world every week."* Refresh as the
  collection grows. Instantly signals "varied but coherent worlds."

## 4. The locked post template (use every single drop)
- **Title:** `[Universe Name] — a [one-word] world`
- **First image = the HERO** (the thumbnail that sells the click).
- **Caption structure:**
  1. Lore caption — 2–3 in-world sentences (from the drop file)
  2. *(blank line)*
  3. One-liner: *"One coherent world — same cast, places, and look. All prompts
     are in the image metadata."*
  4. CTA: *"Built in MorpBase ↓ [link]"*
- **Image order:** Hero → 01 → 10.
- **Metadata:** paste the full prompt **+ the shared negative** into each image's
  generation-data / description field.
- **Tags:** theme (2–3) + `z-image` + content (`original character`,
  `concept art`, `character design`). Exact set is in each drop file.

## 5. Cadence
- **Recommended:** 1 drop/week on a fixed day + time, with an optional **2/week
  launch burst for the first fortnight** to build momentum, then settle to
  weekly.
- You have **5 packs ready** = 5+ weeks of runway already.

## 6. Base model
- **Decided:** Z-Image-Turbo is the brand's primary look. Illustrious is
  reserved for character-spotlight + LoRA drops (e.g. Soraya Vex).

## 8. Metrics sheet (one row per drop)
`Date | Universe | Reactions | Comments | Image downloads | Followers (start→end)
| Profile clicks | MorpBase referral clicks | Notes`
→ After 6 drops, double down on whichever universe aesthetic outperforms.

## 9. Engagement playbook
- Post at peak CivitAI hours; standardise on your best-performing slot.
- Reply to **every** comment in the first ~2 hours — early engagement drives reach.
- **Pin** the best-performing drop to the profile.
- Cross-post each **hero image** to any other channel (X / Discord / Reddit),
  linking back.
- Keep the 2–3 drop buffer rendered ahead (already done).
