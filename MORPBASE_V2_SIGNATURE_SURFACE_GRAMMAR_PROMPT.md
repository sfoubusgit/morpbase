# MorpBase V2 Signature Surface Grammar Prompt

## Goal

Create the `signature surface grammar` for MorpBase V2.

This is the next inevitability artifact after:

- shell inevitability
- realm family design rules

Its job is to define which specific surfaces most determine whether MorpBase feels unmistakably like MorpBase, and what those surfaces must do differently from generic product panels.

## Why This Matters

Even with:

- a stronger shell
- and clearer realm family rules

the product can still feel too prototype-like if its most important surfaces remain:

- merely correct
- merely clean
- or merely pretty

So this phase must answer:

- which surfaces matter most
- what makes them signature surfaces
- and what grammar they should share

## Important Inputs

Use these as the main grounding:

- `MORPBASE_V2_WHOLE_PRODUCT_INEVITABILITY_BRIEF.md`
- `MORPBASE_V2_REALM_FAMILY_DESIGN_RULES.md`
- `MORPBASE_V2_DESIGN_UNIVERSE_PREPARATION.md`
- current coded focus/proof surfaces in `morpbase-v2/src/App.tsx` and `styles.css`

## Required Method

1. restate the real signature-surface gap in plain language
2. use real product moments first
3. compare multiple candidate surface sets
4. choose the strongest signature set
5. define the shared grammar
6. define surface-specific rules
7. explain what should still wait

## Product Moments To Use

At minimum:

1. shaping in `Workspace`
2. trusting `Prompt Preview`
3. deciding to `Keep`
4. focusing a kept line in `Memory`
5. focusing a public result in `Community`
6. focusing a continuity line in `Continuity`

## Candidate Surface Sets To Compare

### Candidate A. Only the center surfaces

Example:

- `Workspace`
- `Prompt Preview`
- `Keep`

### Candidate B. Center plus major focus surfaces

Example:

- `Workspace`
- `Prompt Preview`
- `Keep`
- kept-work focus
- public result focus
- continuity focus

### Candidate C. Nearly every major panel

Example:

- most panels become “signature”

### Candidate D. Focus only on shell and hero areas

Example:

- top frame
- hero surfaces
- leave the rest more generic

## Questions That Must Be Answered

1. Which surfaces most determine MorpBase’s inevitability?
2. What makes a surface feel `signature` instead of just polished?
3. What grammar should these surfaces share?
4. How should each signature surface differ by role?
5. What should still wait until later phases?

## Guardrails

- do not turn every surface into a special surface
- do not flatten roles between center and support surfaces
- do not confuse ornament with identity
- keep `Prompt Preview` and `Keep` special
- keep signature surfaces tied to product meaning

Tone:
