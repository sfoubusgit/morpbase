# MorpBase V2 Signature Surface Grammar

## 1. The Real Signature-Surface Gap

The product already has many good surfaces.

The gap is:

- too many of them still feel like strong panels
- instead of a smaller set of surfaces that clearly carry MorpBase’s identity

Plainly:

- if every important surface is only “good UI,” the product still feels prototype-like

So this phase should not ask:

- how do we make everything more special?

It should ask:

- which surfaces actually define MorpBase
- and what grammar makes them feel unmistakable

## 2. Product Moments

### Shaping in `Workspace`

This should feel like:

- the living heart of the product

### Trusting `Prompt Preview`

This should feel like:

- the place where MorpBase proves itself

### Deciding to `Keep`

This should feel like:

- a meaningful threshold
- not a generic save action

### Focusing a kept line in `Memory`

This should feel like:

- a second home
- a living return point

### Focusing a public result in `Community`

This should feel like:

- public life of real work
- not content display

### Focusing a continuity line in `Continuity`

This should feel like:

- recurring sameness made visible
- not a different tool

## 3. Candidate Comparison

### Candidate A. Only the center surfaces

Good:

- strongest focus

Bad:

- too narrow
- ignores the support surfaces that now matter a lot to the product feeling

Judgment:

- not enough

### Candidate B. Center plus major focus surfaces

Good:

- strongest balance
- captures both the heart and the key supporting value points
- keeps the signature set selective

Judgment:

- strongest option

### Candidate C. Nearly every major panel

Good:

- expressive

Bad:

- weakens what `signature` means
- turns identity into general styling

Judgment:

- wrong move

### Candidate D. Focus only on shell and hero areas

Good:

- efficient

Bad:

- too shallow
- leaves the real product moments too ordinary

Judgment:

- not enough

## 4. Chosen Signature Surface Set

The strongest signature set is:

- **center plus major focus surfaces**

That means the key signature surfaces are:

1. `Workspace`
2. `Prompt Preview`
3. `Keep`
4. kept-work focus surface in `Memory`
5. public result focus surface in `Community`
6. continuity focus surface in `Continuity`

These are the surfaces that most affect whether the product feels like MorpBase.

## 5. Shared Signature Grammar

All signature surfaces should share:

### 1. Stronger frame presence

They should feel more deliberate and more anchored than ordinary panels.

### 2. Clear internal role reading

The surface should immediately communicate what kind of important place it is.

### 3. Stronger proof / value feeling

These surfaces should feel like they hold real value, not just information.

### 4. Shared authored family

They should clearly belong to one MorpBase material language.

### 5. Role-specific gravity

They should carry different weights based on role, not all feel equally emphasized.

## 6. Surface-Specific Rules

### 1. Workspace

Must feel:

- most active
- most alive
- most instrument-like

It should be the clearest making surface in the whole product.

### 2. Prompt Preview

Must feel:

- more than a preview
- like the trust surface

It should feel like where the line becomes believable.

### 3. Keep

Must feel:

- like a threshold
- not like a button row

Its grammar should make it feel meaningful without becoming dramatic.

### 4. Kept-work focus surface

Must feel:

- like a strong return point
- like real preserved creative value

### 5. Public result focus surface

Must feel:

- more outward-facing
- more alive
- more public

But it should still feel like the same product family, not a content page from another app.

### 6. Continuity focus surface

Must feel:

- finer
- quieter
- recurrence-aware

It should feel slightly special without drifting into symbolic theater.

## 7. Non-Signature Surfaces

Not every surface should become signature-rich.

Secondary list, shelf, and management surfaces should remain:

- clear
- supportive
- calmer

This contrast is what helps the signature set matter.

## 8. What This Locks

This locks that MorpBase identity should be carried most strongly through:

- the making surface
- the trust surface
- the threshold surface
- and the main value-focus surfaces across the support realms

Not through:

- every panel becoming equally expressive

## 9. What Should Still Wait

These should still wait:

- full motif system
- heavier symbolic overlays
- final asset-rich art direction
- deep illustration passes

Why:

- the signature surface roles must be settled first

## 10. Honest Conclusion

MorpBase will feel more inevitable when a smaller set of truly important surfaces becomes unmistakably signature-rich. The strongest set is the center plus the major focus surfaces, because those are the places where the product most needs to feel like only MorpBase could work this way.
