# MorpBase V2 Activate Threshold-Mark Pass Review

## Verdict

- **Pass**

## What Improved

`Activate` now feels more like:

- a recurrence crossing back into live work

and less like:

- the final action at the bottom of the continuity panel

### 1. The threshold family now holds across all four core crossings

This is the most important result.

The family now covers:

- `Keep`
- `Publish`
- `Use In Workspace`
- `Activate`

That means the first threshold family is now truly established.

### 2. `Continuity` stayed disciplined

This mattered a lot.

The pass did not make `Continuity` feel:

- mystical
- special-power driven
- or more theatrical than the rest of the product

It still feels:

- lighter
- finer
- and product-real

### 3. The activation path now feels more native

The continuity focus now says more clearly:

- recurrence can cross into fresh creation

That strengthens the role of `Continuity` as a real but secondary return layer.

## What Still Is Not Final

This is still the coded bridge, not the finished custom threshold family.

The later Illustrator-backed layer will still want:

- finer bracket refinement
- stronger family consistency
- more exact line quality
- and quieter custom node behavior

## What This Pass Did Not Break

Important:

- `Continuity` did not become louder
- the product did not drift into symbolic theater
- the threshold family still feels restrained
- the family still belongs to the same product world

## Recommended Next Move

The healthiest next move is:

- **threshold family checkpoint and consolidation**

Why:

- the first family is now complete
- this is the right stage boundary to reassess the whole set
- and it would be unhealthy to keep pushing into more symbolic passes without a broader judgment

## Honest Conclusion

This was a good fourth threshold-family bridge because it proved the family can reach `Continuity` without losing MorpBase composure.
