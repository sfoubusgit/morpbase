# MorpBase V2 Scene Pressure Layer Prompt

Design the first light `scene-pressure` layer inside `Stage The Image` for MorpBase V2.

The goal is:

- make Phase 3 feel less compressed
- add one second staging dimension beside framing
- keep the center calm and guided

Focus on:

- how the line meets the viewer
- the felt pressure or stillness of the image
- what helps staging feel stronger without turning into scene design

Judge:

- how many scene-pressure options are healthy
- whether they should be broad chips or grouped
- how they should relate to framing

Avoid:

- background-building
- full composition design
- environment libraries
- narrative prompts

Prefer:

- a small curated field
- clear difference from look-language
- clear usefulness to the preview
