# MorpBase V2 Prototype Phase A To E Checkpoint Prompt

Use this prompt after the full first-wave V2 prototype program has been completed through:

- Phase A: Workspace
- Phase B: Memory
- Phase C: Community
- Phase D: Continuity
- Phase E: Whole-product synthesis

Your job is to step back from the separate slices and judge what the full prototype program has actually taught us about MorpBase V2.

## Core Goal

Reassess everything learned so far and decide:

- what still clearly holds
- what became weaker
- what should be recombined more strongly
- what mistakes or drift risks are still visible
- whether a better overall reading of the product has emerged
- and what the real next phase should be

## Main Questions

Answer these directly:

1. What is the strongest whole-product truth the full prototype program proved?
2. What parts of the earlier V2 thinking now look stronger than before?
3. What parts still look too weak, too early, or too abstract?
4. What should now be recombined into a cleaner master reading?
5. What future ideas now look more plausible:
   - sharability
   - collaboration
   - playfulness
   - challenges
   - storylines
   - reward
6. What still must not happen if V2 is to avoid the V1 mistake?
7. What is the real next phase after the prototype program?

## Required Output

Write a concise but serious reassessment that includes:

- executive conclusion

## Important Rules

- Be willing to correct earlier thinking.
- Judge the total product, not isolated slice quality.
- Keep asking whether the product feels integrated.
- Do not drift into implementation detail yet.
- Keep the heart of MorpBase central:
  - workflow-centered creation
  - memory
  - real reuse
  - recurring sameness
  - public circulation that feeds value back inward
