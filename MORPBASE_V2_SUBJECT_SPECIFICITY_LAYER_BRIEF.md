# MorpBase V2 Subject-Specificity Layer Brief

## Intent

This step should make the chosen subject feel more particular earlier in the flow.

The problem is not:

- lack of subject breadth anymore

The problem is:

- the chosen subject can still feel a bit too archetypal until too late

## Scope

Design only:

- the first light specificity layer inside Phase 1

Do not design:

- a large freeform prompt area
- more subject taxonomies
- new workflow phases
- new edge-system growth

## Main Goal

Keep Phase 1:

- light
- guided
- curated

But make it:

- more particular
- earlier
- and less generic

## Honest Standard

If this works, the next coded center slice should make the subject feel:

- chosen
- and then quickly made more specific

instead of staying archetype-only until the very end
