# MorpBase V2 Coded Design Translation Brief

## Purpose

This phase should make the current coded V2 feel more like **real MorpBase** and less like a disciplined prototype.

The structure is now strong enough.
The next gain should come from stronger expression.

## What This Phase Should Strengthen

### 1. Shell identity

The shell should feel more characteristic, more confident, and more unmistakably MorpBase.

### 2. Hierarchy through visual force

`Workspace`, `Memory`, `Community`, and `Continuity` should not only be structurally different.
They should feel different in the right hierarchy through visual weight and atmosphere.

### 3. Object-carrying feel

The product should feel more clearly like the same meaningful work is moving through different states of value.

### 4. Product atmosphere

The coded V2 should feel more crafted, more alive, and less like an early working shell.

## Recommended Design Translation Areas

### 1. Top-level shell and realm identity

Strengthen the personality of the shell itself.

### 2. Cross-realm bridge surfaces

Make return paths and carried work feel more visually intentional.

### 3. Object focus surfaces

Strengthen the feeling that focus areas in `Memory`, `Community`, and `Continuity` are part of one product family.

### 4. Preview and proof surfaces

Make `Prompt Preview` and other proof-oriented surfaces feel more trustworthy and more signature.

### 5. Early shape-language decisions

Introduce stronger recurring shape language, panel behavior, and atmospheric cues that later custom design work can build on.

## What Must Stay Out

- no new product systems
- no final Illustrator-heavy art pass yet
- no fake polish detached from product meaning
- no flattening of the product into a cleaner-but-more-generic app

## Success Criteria

This phase succeeds if the coded V2 feels:

- more distinct
- more product-real
- more coherent
- more unmistakably like MorpBase

without needing new structural expansion first
