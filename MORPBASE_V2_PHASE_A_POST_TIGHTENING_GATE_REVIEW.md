# MorpBase V2 Phase A Post-Tightening Gate Review

## Executive Judgment

**Current judgment: `Pass`**

The tightening pass fixed the most important Phase A problems.

This prototype now feels much closer to the intended V2 experience:

- the Workspace is clearly the center
- the left side feels more guided
- the Preview feels more readable at a glance
- the keep/save moment feels more natural

It is still a prototype, and some review-only scaffolding remains.
But the core feel is now strong enough that Phase A can be treated as closed.

## What Improved Clearly

### 1. The left side feels lighter

This is the biggest improvement.

Before, seeing all seven blocks fully open at once made the Workspace feel a bit too much like a structured setup form.

Now:

- the active block gets the focus
- later blocks feel quieter
- the experience is easier to enter

That makes the Workspace feel more guided without becoming a wizard.

### 2. Preview now builds confidence faster

The additional visual signals help.

The Preview is still calm, but it now gives a better “this is taking shape” feeling at a glance.
That is better than relying mostly on explanatory text.

### 3. The keep/save moment feels more organic

The save surface now appears in a softer way:

- first as “almost worth keeping”
- then as a real keep/save moment

That is much closer to the product behavior we want.
It feels less like a state machine and more like a result gaining value.

### 4. The shell still works

This stayed strong through the iteration:

- `Workspace` feels dominant
- `Memory` and `Community` stay visible
- `Continuity` stays lighter
- `Profile` stays utility-level

That asymmetry still feels correct.

## What Still Feels Weak

### 1. The review dock is still obviously a prototype tool

That is acceptable for now, but it should never influence the real product.

### 2. The Preview could become even more emotionally convincing later

It is now good enough for Phase A, but later higher-fidelity prototype work could still make the right side feel richer and more “worth keeping.”

### 3. The flow still depends on simplified prototype logic

That is also acceptable for now.
At this phase, the question is not whether the logic is production-grade.
The question is whether the product feel is correct.

## Final Gate Call

**Final judgment: `Pass`**

Phase A is now good enough to close.

The prototype has proven the most important V2 truth:

- the first-use Workspace can feel clear, guided, trustworthy, and connected to future value

That is enough to stop iterating this slice for now.

## Recommendation

Move to the next prototype slice:

- `Phase B: Memory continuation`

That is now the right next step, because the strongest remaining unanswered question is no longer:

- “does the Workspace center work?”

It is:

- “does the jump from Workspace into Memory keep the product feeling alive and coherent?”
