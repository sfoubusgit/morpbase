The coded V2 now holds as one real integrated product strongly enough to stop local center growth.

- **whole-product proportion**

### 1. `Workspace` now clearly deserves to be the center

- continuation
- living return bridge

### 3. `Community` feels real enough

- public object circulation
- creator grounding
- publishing
- response traces

That means it no longer needs immediate growth to feel believable.

### 4. `Continuity` is still appropriately lighter

It remains:

- real
- useful
- secondary

That is the right role for this stage.

## Where The Next Risk Is

The next risk is not:

- missing systems

It is:

- **support realms pulling too evenly against the now-stronger center**

Plainly:

- the product is strongest when `Workspace` is unmistakably the core
- but some support surfaces can still feel a little too equally present once the center becomes stronger

This is not a feature problem.

It is a:

- proportion
- calmness
- and hierarchy-expression problem

## The Single Healthiest Next Move

The strongest next move is:

- **support-realm proportion tightening**

Meaning:

- keep `Memory`, `Community`, and `Continuity` real
- but make their secondary status read more clearly beside the stronger center

## Why This Wins

This wins because it protects the whole-product truth:

- one creative engine
- with support realms that return value into creation

It is stronger than:

- more Community growth
- more Continuity growth
- or reopening the center again

## What This Step Should Target

It should target:

- support-surface force
- realm weighting
- cross-realm hierarchy expression
- how strongly secondary realms compete for attention

Not:

- more objects
- more features
- or more subsystems

## Plain Conclusion

The next disciplined move is:

- tighten whole-product proportion so the support realms clearly support the center instead of competing with it
