# MorpBase V2 Post-Validation Live Inspection Prompt

Goal:
- run one fresh whole-product inspection after the first validation round

What to judge:
- does the current V2 now feel more inevitable as one product
- does `Workspace` still clearly dominate without flattening the support realms
- do `Memory`, `Community`, and `Continuity` still feel distinct but integrated
- does anything still feel too app-like, too neat, or too theoretical

Inspection lenses:
- center strength
- support-realm balance
- public inward meaning
- continuity payoff
- overall authored product feel

Guardrails:
- stay at whole-product level
- do not reopen local solved questions without a real signal
- only open a tuning pass if something feels directly wrong

Success condition:
- the live baseline receives one honest post-validation reading
- the next move becomes clear from that reading
