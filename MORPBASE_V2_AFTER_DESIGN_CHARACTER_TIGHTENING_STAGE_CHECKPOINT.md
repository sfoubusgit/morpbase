# MorpBase V2 After Design-Character Tightening Stage Checkpoint

## 1. What Now Clearly Holds

- the coded V2 now feels much more like one authored MorpBase product
- the shell is strong enough to hold the product's current complexity
- `Community` now feels more intentionally subordinate to the larger engine
- `Memory` still reads as the strongest bridge layer
- `Continuity` still reads as lighter but real
- the total product now has stronger hierarchy, atmosphere, and identity than before

The strongest current truth is:

- the product edges are now better held by the product center

That is a meaningful improvement.

## 2. What Still Needs Protection

- `Workspace` must remain the emotional and structural center
- `Memory` must remain the strongest bridge layer
- public participation must remain an extension, not a competing center
- future design work must still stay meaning-led, not decorative
- the product must not restart edge-growth now that the shell is stronger

The biggest risk now is:

- thinking the stronger shell means it is safe to keep broadening outward again

That would undo the value of this phase.

## 3. Biggest Remaining Gap

The biggest remaining gap is now:

- **center depth**

In plain terms:

- the product shell is stronger
- the public side is richer
- but the actual `Workspace` creation model is still narrower than the long-term V2 vision

That means the next meaningful gain should come from:

- deepening the center

not:

- enlarging the edges

## 4. Best Next Move

The healthiest next move is:

- a `Workspace depth and center-growth decision` phase

Meaning:

- step back into the core
- decide what the next controlled `Workspace` growth slice should be
- make sure the center grows from the stronger product base now in place

The strongest likely candidates should come from things like:

- richer authoring guidance
- a stronger internal workflow block model
- a deeper proof/preview relationship
- or better shaped continuation from `Memory` into live authoring

What should still explicitly wait:

- challenges
- rewards
- events
- storylines
- bigger public/community systems

## 5. Honest Conclusion

`After the design-character tightening phase, MorpBase V2 is now in a healthier state: the shell holds the current product better, the public side no longer feels as close to becoming a second center, and the strongest next move is to return to the heart of the product and decide the next controlled Workspace growth slice rather than broadening outward again.`
