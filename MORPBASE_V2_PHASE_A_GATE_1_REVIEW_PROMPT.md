## Review Goal

Determine whether Phase A:

- passes Gate 1
- passes conditionally and needs iteration
- or fails and must be reworked before Phase B

## Review Method

Use the real prototype artifact, not just the docs.

Judge it against the real Gate 1 criteria:

1. The user immediately understands where to begin
2. The guided first-flow layer feels embedded, not tutorial-like
3. The first shaping move feels real, not form-like
4. `Prompt Preview` builds trust early
5. `Keep` / `Save` feels earned and meaningful
6. The handoff toward `Memory` feels natural
7. The shell preserves center hierarchy

## Required Output

Write the result to:

- `MORPBASE_V2_PHASE_A_GATE_1_REVIEW.md`

The review should contain:

1. Executive judgment
2. Criterion-by-criterion assessment
3. Strongest successes
4. Most important weaknesses
5. Prototype-only artifacts that should not become product behavior by accident
6. Recommended iteration list in order
7. Final gate judgment:
   - `Pass`
   - `Conditional Pass`
   - `Fail`

## Important Constraints

- Be honest and critical
- Do not protect work just because it already exists
- Distinguish between:
  - acceptable prototype scaffolding
  - dangerous structural drift
- Do not jump to Phase B if Phase A still has center-of-gravity problems
