# MorpBase — CivitAI Creator-Brand Campaign (Campaign 01)

## Strategy in one line
Become **the worldbuilding account** on CivitAI: drop a new, fully-coherent *universe*
on a steady cadence, share the prompts, and let the **consistency itself** be the proof
that MorpBase works.

## The hook (why anyone follows)
Most AI galleries are scattershot — a random face here, a random landscape there. Ours are
**worlds**: every image in a drop shares one coherent universe — the same cast, places,
look, and mood. That coherence is the differentiator *and* the implicit MorpBase pitch.
Followers come back because each drop is a new immersive world they can explore and reuse.

## Brand identity
- **Account/persona:** pick one consistent handle + avatar + banner. Working name options:
  *MorpBase Worlds*, *The Worldsmith*, *Atlas of Made Worlds*. (Decide before Drop 1.)
- **Voice:** confident worldbuilder/curator. Lore-forward, restrained, no "🔥 masterpiece 8k"
  hype. Let the worlds talk.
- **Visual signature:** each universe is aesthetically distinct, but the *post format* is
  identical every time (title card → lore line → consistent set → shared prompts → one CTA).
- **Bio:** one-line pitch + link. e.g. *"I build coherent worlds, then share the prompts. Made in MorpBase →"*

## Content pillars
1. **Universe Drops** (core, ~80%) — a cohesive 8–12 image set from one universe.
2. **Character Spotlights** — one recurring character, multiple looks/scenes.
3. **LoRAs** (occasional hero assets) — downloadable; **Soraya Vex** is the first.
4. **Articles** (~monthly) — "how this whole world stayed consistent" → method → MorpBase.

## The Drop format (repeatable template)
Every universe drop ships as one CivitAI **post**:
- **1 hero / title image** (the most striking, establishes the world)
- **8–12 images**, consistent cast/place/style, each with the **full prompt in metadata**
- **Title:** `[Universe Name] — a [one-word genre] world`
- **Lore caption:** 2–3 sentences of in-world flavor (no marketing-speak)
- **Tag set:** universe theme + base model (Z-Image-Turbo / Illustrious) + 2–3 style tags
- **One CTA line** at the end: *"Built as one coherent world in MorpBase — prompts shared above."*

> Rule: share the prompts openly. Goodwill + "made in MorpBase" on every prompt is the funnel.

## The MorpBase funnel (soft, never spammy)
Three light touchpoints, no hard sell:
1. **Profile bio link** to MorpBase.
2. **One CTA line** per post + "made in MorpBase" on shared prompts.
3. The **monthly Article** is the real conversion piece (explains the method, links the tool).
Sharing prompts freely is the strategy, not a giveaway — it demonstrates the tool and earns follows.

## Discoverability / tags
Per drop, combine: **theme** (e.g. cyberpunk, folklore, gothic) + **base model** tag +
**style** (e.g. neon noir, oil painting) + **content** (original character, concept art,
character design). Consistent theme tags help the "collection" read as a body of work.

## Engagement tactics
- Post at peak CivitAI hours; reply to every early comment.
- Cross-post the hero image to any MorpBase channels (Discord/X) pointing back.
- Keep a 2–3 drop buffer rendered ahead so cadence never slips.
- Pin the best-performing drop on the profile.

## Metrics (track per drop, simple sheet)
Reactions · new follows · image downloads · profile clicks · MorpBase referral clicks.
After 6 drops, double down on whichever *universe aesthetic* outperforms.
