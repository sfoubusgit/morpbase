# MorpBase V2 After Creator Grounding Public-Side Checkpoint

## What Now Clearly Holds

- `Community` no longer feels like anonymous public storage
- public objects now have stronger meaning, consequence, and invitation
- creator grounding gives the public side a more human center of gravity
- the public loop now feels closer to:
  - release
  - discover
  - understand through practice
  - bring inward again

The strongest new truth is:

- the public side now feels like real public creative practice, not just public entries

## What Still Needs Protection

- objects must remain the center of Community
- creator grounding must stay practice-led, not profile-first
- the public side must still resist feed drift
- impact and invitation layers must stay calm
- the product must not drift into admin-console behavior

The biggest caution is:

- if the next step is too managerial, Community could lose the public-life feeling it just gained

## Biggest Remaining Public-Side Gap

The biggest remaining gap is now:

- **Publishing**

In plain terms:

- Discover now exists
- creator grounding now exists
- but the public side still lacks a real release-and-management bridge inside Community itself

Right now public life mostly starts from `Memory` actions.

That works, but it is not yet enough to make Community feel fully complete as:

- Discover
- Creators
- Publishing

So the gap is not:

- more creator identity
- more social mechanics
- or more discovery energy first

It is:

- a calm place where private objects can be seen as ready for public life
- released thoughtfully
- and managed once public

## Best Next Move

The healthiest next move is:

- **a calm Publishing slice inside Community**

The first version should be:

- a public circulation desk
- not a heavy dashboard
- not an upload page
- and not a second studio

What it should prove:

- what is ready to release
- what is already public
- what can be withdrawn or reopened
- and how private value moves into public life

This is the strongest next move because it completes the current Community triangle:

- Discover
- Creators
- Publishing

without introducing a new product logic.

## Why The Other Options Should Wait

### More Creator Growth

Useful later, but the current creator layer is already enough to support the next step.

### More Discover Vitality

Also useful later, but Discover now needs Publishing to make the public loop feel complete.

### Early Collaboration Mechanics

Too early.

The public side still needs its release-and-management bridge before shared work mechanics.

### Early Challenge / Playfulness Mechanics

Too early for the same reason.

They need a stronger public loop first.

## Honest Conclusion

`After creator grounding, the healthiest next V2 public-side step is a calm Publishing slice that completes the public circulation loop without turning Community into an admin dashboard or a second creative studio.`
