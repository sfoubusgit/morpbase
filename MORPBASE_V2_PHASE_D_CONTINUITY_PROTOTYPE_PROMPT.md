# MorpBase V2 Phase D Continuity Prototype Prompt

## Purpose

Define the prototype brief for `Phase D: Continuity activation`.

This slice must not prototype Continuity as:

- a broad story/world system
- a generic identity-management app
- a backstage metadata screen
- or a decorative realm with no real payoff

It must prototype Continuity as:

- a lighter but real MorpBase realm
- centered on recurring entities
- grounded by appearances through work
- and proven through activation back into `Workspace`

## Required Inputs

Use and respect:

- `MORPBASE_V2_FOUNDATION_FREEZE.md`
- `MORPBASE_V2_PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md`
- `MORPBASE_V2_INTERACTION_BLUEPRINT.md`
- `MORPBASE_V2_PROTOTYPE_EXECUTION_WORKFLOW.md`
- `MORPBASE_V2_CONTINUITY_REALM_MODEL_ANALYSIS.md`
- `MORPBASE_V2_IMAGE_ROLE_ANALYSIS.md`
- `IDENTITY_SYSTEMS_REALM_ANALYSIS.md`

## Core Question

What is the smallest prototype slice that can prove:

- Continuity is concrete enough to matter
- recurring entities feel real
- appearances/lineage feel understandable
- activation visibly changes `Workspace`
- and the realm still stays lighter than the core product

## What Phase D Must Prove

The brief must define a slice that proves:

1. `Continuity` feels real, not decorative
2. an `Entity` feels meaningfully different from saved work or reusable assets
3. `Appearances` make recurrence understandable
4. images support continuity as evidence, not as a gallery system
5. activation into `Workspace` feels meaningful
6. Continuity still feels lighter than `Workspace`, `Memory`, and `Community`

## Slice Discipline

Do not let this slice become:

- the whole future of Identity Systems
- a collaboration/storyline system
- a worldbuilding prototype
- a full continuity dashboard

But do preserve light headroom for:

- later valid entity lanes
- later relationship growth
- later storyline evolution

## Output

Write:

- `MORPBASE_V2_PHASE_D_CONTINUITY_PROTOTYPE_BRIEF.md`

The brief must include:

1. executive conclusion
2. what Phase D must prove
3. the main user journey
4. minimum artifact set
5. core prototype states
6. critical transitions
7. what must feel live vs what can stay mocked
8. Gate 4 review criteria
9. strongest failure signs
10. the next artifact recommended after the brief
