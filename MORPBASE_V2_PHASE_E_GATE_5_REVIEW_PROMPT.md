# MorpBase V2 Phase E Gate 5 Review Prompt

Use this prompt after the first Phase E whole-product synthesis prototype exists.

Your job is to judge whether the stitched prototype proves that MorpBase V2 feels like one integrated creative engine, or whether it still feels too much like several strong parts placed beside each other.

## Required Inputs

Read these first:

- `MORPBASE_V2_PHASE_E_WHOLE_PRODUCT_SYNTHESIS_BRIEF.md`
- `MORPBASE_V2_PHASE_E_WHOLE_PRODUCT_FLOW_MAP.md`
- `MORPBASE_V2_PROTOTYPE_PHASE_A_TO_D_CHECKPOINT_REASSESSMENT.md`
- `MORPBASE_V2_IMAGE_ROLE_ANALYSIS.md`

Also inspect the Phase E prototype itself:

- `v2-prototypes/phase-e-whole-product/index.html`
- `v2-prototypes/phase-e-whole-product/styles.css`
- `v2-prototypes/phase-e-whole-product/script.js`

## Main Questions

Answer these directly:

1. Does the stitched journey feel like one engine?
2. Does `Workspace` still clearly feel like the center even after multiple realm shifts?
3. Does `Memory` still feel like the strongest second home?
4. Do `Community` and `Continuity` feel integrated rather than decorative or detached?
5. Do images still act as proof rather than center?
6. Do future ideas like sharability, collaboration, challenges, and storylines feel plausible as later attachment points without inflating the product now?

## Required Output

Write a concise Gate 5 review that includes:

- overall judgment:
  - `Pass`
  - `Conditional Pass`
  - or `Fail`
- what is strongest
- what still feels weak
- what changed in the total product reading
- the single most important next move

Save the result as:

- `MORPBASE_V2_PHASE_E_GATE_5_REVIEW.md`

## Important Rules

- Be honest.
- Do not reward the prototype for merely restating correct product ideas.
- Judge what feels integrated versus what still feels assembled.
- Prioritize the lived whole-product feeling over isolated screen quality.
