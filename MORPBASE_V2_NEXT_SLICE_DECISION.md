# MorpBase V2 Next Slice Decision

## Chosen Next Slice

`Community public circulation`

## Why It Is Next

Now that the private core loop is strong enough for the moment, the next most valuable thing to prove is sharability without losing the center.

`Community` is the right next slice because it tests whether MorpBase work can begin to live publicly while still returning value back into the same engine.

It is stronger next than `Continuity` because:

- sharability is a more immediate product pressure
- it broadens the product without taking over the center
- it helps prove that MorpBase can become a real product people return to, not just a private tool

## What It Should Prove

- public circulation can grow naturally out of real kept work
- shared work can still feel like MorpBase objects, not generic posts
- the path from `Memory` into public life can be meaningful
- the path back from public life into personal use can stay clear

## What Should Still Wait

- `Continuity` implementation
- collaboration systems
- reward systems
- challenges
- storylines
- deeper creator/profile layers
