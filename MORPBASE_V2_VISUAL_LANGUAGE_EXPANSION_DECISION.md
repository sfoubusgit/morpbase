# MorpBase V2 Visual Language Expansion Decision

## Decision

The first `Shape The Look` expansion should widen:

- `Visual Direction`

through:

- one curated grouped field

It should **not** widen into:

- a style library
- a trend catalog
- a long flat list

## What This Means

`Phase 2` now uses three calm look families:

- `Crafted Lines`
- `Light Lines`
- `Screen Lines`

With six total look directions:

- `Painterly`
- `Illustrative`
- `Luminous`
- `Nocturne`
- `Cinematic`
- `Documentary`

## Why This Wins

The subject side had already become stronger through:

- broader entry
- earlier particularity

So the next weak spot was:

- the narrowness of the look field

This move fixes that directly without making the center feel more technical.

## Mood Position

`Mood And Light` stays broader and lighter on purpose.

That keeps the structure clear:

- look direction shapes the image language
- mood pressures the image without competing with the look field

## Plain Conclusion

This is the right first Phase 2 deepening move because it makes the look side feel:

- more believable
- more expressive
- still guided
