# MorpBase V2 Starter-Kit Baseline Checkpoint

## What Now Clearly Holds

- V2 now has a real first custom-asset baseline
- the shell family is materially executable
- the threshold family is materially executable
- the trace family is materially executable

The strongest new truth is:

- MorpBase now has a usable first design-universe bridge, not just coded proof

That matters because the product now has:

- working placement
- meaningful family logic
- restrained starter vectors
- and a repeatable handoff pattern

## What Still Needs Protection

- shell assets must stay structural
- threshold assets must stay tied to real crossings only
- trace assets must stay quieter than thresholds
- the asset layer must not spread into generic UI too early
- the product must still get its strength mainly from hierarchy and flow, not from asset density

The biggest protection rule now is:

- the starter kits should remain a baseline, not become pressure to decorate more of the product automatically

## Biggest Remaining Gap

The biggest remaining gap is now:

- **actual refinement or deliberate freeze**

In plain terms:

- the kits are ready
- but they are still starter vectors

So the real missing choice is:

- refine them now in Illustrator

or:

- freeze them as the first baseline and continue later from a clean stop point

## Best Next Move

The healthiest next move is:

- **baseline freeze plus deliberate choice**

Meaning:

- lock the current shell, threshold, and trace kits as the first executable baseline
- give the design-assets area one clear root entrypoint
- and then choose whether Illustrator refinement starts now or later

What should still wait:

- more asset families
- more decorative spread
- broader illustration work

## Honest Conclusion

`The three starter kits now hold well enough to count as the first real custom-asset baseline for MorpBase V2. The healthiest next move is to freeze that baseline cleanly, then deliberately choose whether to begin actual Illustrator refinement or hold this state as the current design-universe handoff point.`
