# MorpBase V2 Support Realm Proportion Tightening Decision

## Decision

The right move is a restrained design pass across `Memory`, `Community`, and `Continuity`.

## What This Pass Should Do

- keep all three support realms real
- keep their main focus surfaces meaningful
- reduce how equally forceful they feel beside `Workspace`
- express that they support a stronger center rather than compete with it

## What This Pass Should Not Do

- add new features
- weaken `Memory`
- flatten the product into one generic surface
- make the support realms feel empty or unimportant

## Chosen Design Move

Tighten proportion mainly through:

- calmer support-realm shell force
- calmer focus-surface intensity
- softer banners and support blocks
- slightly reduced internal framing and glow

## Intended Product Reading

If this works, the product should feel more like:

- one strong creative engine with real support layers

instead of:

- several similarly weighted product zones
