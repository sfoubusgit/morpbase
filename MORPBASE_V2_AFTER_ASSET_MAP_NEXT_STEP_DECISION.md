# MorpBase V2 After Asset Map Next Step Decision

## Decision

After the asset map, the healthiest next move is:

- **threshold mark execution brief**

## Why This Wins

Thresholds are:

- highly MorpBase-native
- visible in the product already
- and strong enough to benefit from clearer custom language next

They also connect well to:

- `Keep`
- `Publish`
- `Use In Workspace`
- `Activate`

So they are the best bridge between:

- the current coded V2
- and the future Illustrator-backed custom layer

## Why Not Another Step First

### Not a full trace system yet

Because traces are important, but thresholds are clearer and simpler as the first custom execution family.

### Not wider shell work first

Because the shell just had a good pass already.

### Not signature-surface framing first

Because those surfaces will benefit from threshold language once it exists.

## Plain Conclusion

The next clean move is:

- define the custom threshold language first

That is the most grounded way to keep moving without drifting.
