# MorpBase V2 Broader Center Inspection Result

## Status

- **Pass with one justified next center move**

## What The Broader Subject Field Solved

The broader subject field clearly helped.

It made `Define The Subject` feel:

- less prototype-thin
- more believable
- more like a real creative field

The grouped subject structure was the right choice because it feels:

- curated
- shaped
- broader without feeling generic

So the earlier problem:

- "the center feels too narrow too early"

is now much weaker.

## What Now Feels Stronger

### 1. The center feels more alive

The first phase now feels more like:

- a real creative entry

and less like:

- three starter buttons

### 2. The broader field still stays readable

The grouped subject options did **not** break the calm rhythm.

That matters a lot.

The center still feels:

- curated
- approachable
- product-real

### 3. The center is now stronger than before without becoming heavier

That is the most important success of this slice.

## What Still Feels Thin

The next thinness is no longer:

- subject breadth

It is:

- **subject particularity early in the flow**

Plainly:

- the center is broader now
- but Phase 1 is still a bit too archetype-led

Right now, real particularity still arrives too late, because the free note lives in:

- `Refine And Keep`

That means the subject can still feel:

- selected

before it feels:

- truly particular

## The Single Healthiest Next Center Move

The strongest next center move is:

- **a light subject-specificity layer inside Phase 1**

Meaning:

- keep the broader curated subject field
- but add one very small way to make the subject more particular early

This should be:

- light
- optional
- and specific

Not:

- a big freeform prompt box
- a taxonomy
- or another heavy block

## Why This Wins

This wins because it solves the new center gap directly:

- broader entry already exists
- now the line needs earlier particularity

It is stronger than:

- adding even more subject options
- widening visual options next
- or adding another big center block

## What Should Still Wait

These should still wait:

- more subject libraries
- custom subject systems
- open-ended taxonomies
- more public-side growth
- more continuity growth

## Honest Conclusion

The broader center slice was the right move.

The next healthy center step is not:

- more breadth again

It is:

- earlier subject particularity

That is now the clearest next way to deepen the heart of MorpBase without reopening drift.
