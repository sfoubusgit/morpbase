# MorpBase V2 Support-Realm Feel Freeze Brief

Goal:
- protect the current support-realm feel as part of the active V2 baseline

What is now frozen:
- `Memory` as a second home for living work
- `Community` as object-led public life around real work
- `Continuity` as light but real recurrence

What selective tuning still allows:
- calmness tightening if a support surface grows too loud
- hierarchy tightening if a support realm pulls too evenly against `Workspace`
- wording cleanup if a realm starts sounding generic or mechanical

What this freeze blocks:
- support-side growth by momentum
- new public participation layers by reflex
- heavier continuity management behavior

Success condition:
- support realms stay valuable without diluting the stronger center
