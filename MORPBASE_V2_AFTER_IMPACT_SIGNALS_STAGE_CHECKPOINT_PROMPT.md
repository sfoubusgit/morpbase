# MorpBase V2 After Impact Signals Stage Checkpoint Prompt

## Goal

Reassess the coded V2 after the first meaningful sharing and impact-signals slice.

This checkpoint should decide:

- what the product now does better
- what new risks appeared
- what still needs protection
- and what the healthiest next controlled growth step really is

## Starting Point

The coded V2 now has:

- `Workspace`
- `Memory`
- `Community`
- `Continuity`
- `Kept Work`
- `Reusable Assets`
- `Public Workflow Results`
- `Public Reusable Assets`
- calm impact lines on major object focus surfaces

The current product can now show:

- kept
- released
- imported
- reused
- carried forward

## Method

### 1. Reassess The Whole Engine

Do not judge the new slice in isolation.

Ask:

- does the whole V2 now feel more meaningful?
- or more tracked?
- more alive?
- or more system-heavy?

### 2. Look For New Drift

Specifically check for:

- metric drift
- badge drift
- too much object explanation
- too many layered product concepts
- loss of `Workspace` as center

### 3. Compare The Most Likely Next Directions

At minimum compare:

- a light sharing-impact refinement pass
- object invitations as the next bridge
- early collaboration mechanics
- early challenge/playfulness mechanics
- early creator/public-social expansion

## Main Questions

### 1. What Now Clearly Holds?

- What got stronger?
- What is now more believable?

### 2. What Still Needs Protection?

- What could still drift?
- What must remain disciplined?

### 3. What Is The Biggest Remaining Gap Now?

- What does the product still not do well enough?

### 4. What Is The Best Next Move?

- Which next step strengthens the same engine without starting a new mini-product?

## Output Format

Write the result as:

1. `What Now Clearly Holds`
2. `What Still Needs Protection`
3. `Biggest Remaining Gap`
4. `Best Next Move`
5. `Why The Other Options Should Wait`

## Guardrails

- do not reopen the whole product foundation
- do not recommend collaboration or challenges unless they now clearly fit
- do not let the product become metric-shaped
- keep the answer grounded in the coded V2, not only in theory
